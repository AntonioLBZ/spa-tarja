# Shared expenses

An entry of a group is an expense split between its members, or a settlement ([balances](balances.md)). The decisions are in [ADR-0012](../adr/0012-shared-expenses.md) and [ADR-0013](../adr/0013-shared-entries-sync-through-an-outbox.md).

## Rules

- **The entry.** `id` (a UUID made on the client, read in lower case like the ids of groups and members), `kind` (`expense` or `settlement`), `text` (up to 200 characters), `categoryId` (an expense only), `amountCents` (1 to `MAX_AMOUNT_CENTS`), `currency` (the group's), `date` (`YYYY-MM-DD`), `paidBy` (a member id), `split` (`equal`, `percent` or `exact`), `shares` (a list of `{ memberId, cents }`, at most 50, `MAX_SHARES`: more than a group's members can be over time, those who left and merged included), `payment` (`direct`, the default, `credit` or `voucher`) and `chargeDate`, `createdAt`, `updatedAt` and `deletedAt` (epoch milliseconds, safe integers, so they are stored exactly). The server records who made it and who wrote it last (`created_by` and `updated_by`, user ids from the session, never from the client) and its `seq`; the answers carry those two people as their members in the group (`createdBy` and `updatedBy`, null once that person deleted their account), never a user id. The shape is validated in `core/` by one schema the client and the server share (`core/shared-entry.ts`), with these checks: the shares name each member once, each has at least one cent, they add up to `amountCents` exactly, a settlement has no category, has a single share, is not paid to oneself, is paid `direct` and is split `exact` (its one share is the amount), a `credit` or `voucher` expense has a `chargeDate` not before `date` and a share of the payer of at least one cent, and a `direct` one has no `chargeDate` (a shared expense has no installments). A `payment` left out is `direct` and a `chargeDate` left out is none. The schema checks only the shape of the category; that it is a default one is checked apart, with `isDefaultCategoryId` (`core/categories.ts`, the list the default categories are defined in), so the server can answer `unknown-category` (below).
- **Who paid** is one member who hasn't left; the person who is logging it by default, but anybody can record that another member paid. Several payers are left for later; they would be a new nullable column that overrides `paidBy`.
- **Splitting** is done on the device, in cents, in `core/split.ts`, and what it works out is stored as `shares`; the mode is stored only so the form opens as it was left.
    - **Equally** among the members who are in (everybody, at the start): each gets the amount divided, and the cents left go one at a time to members chosen at random when the expense is made, not the first ones in the group's order nor the person adding it (installments still give the remainder to the first parts, [ADR-0004](../adr/0004-data-conventions.md)). The choice is a seeded shuffle of the members, so `core/split.ts` stays pure and the same seed always gives the same shares: the form draws one 32-bit seed when it opens (`crypto.getRandomValues`, which also works outside a secure context) and keeps it while it is open, so the amounts it shows don't move as the amount or the description is typed, and the shares it saves are the ones it showed; a new form, and the form emptied after an expense is added, draws a new one. The seed is never stored: the shares are the truth, and the server only validates them, so any valid shares are accepted whoever got the cents. An edit keeps the cents saved while the split isn't touched. A member whose part comes to nothing (an amount of fewer cents than the members who are in) is left out of the shares, since a share is at least one cent, and which one is left out is chosen the same way.
    - **By percent**: each member who is in takes a percentage with up to two decimals, 0 to 100, which must add up to 100. A part is the amount times the percentage, rounded down; the cents that are left go one at a time to the biggest fractions, and on a tie to the member the seed of the form puts first. A member at 0 is left out, and so is one whose part comes to nothing. The device works in hundredths of a percent, whole numbers as the cents are (`HUNDRED_PERCENT` is 10 000).
    - **By amount**: each member who is in takes an exact amount. The form shows what is still unassigned and refuses to save until it is zero, and refuses a part that goes over the total, like the installments.
    - **Leaving someone out** is unchecking them: their share is zero and they aren't in the list of shares. At least one member has to be in. The payer can be left out (paying for the others).
    - Changing the mode keeps who is in. Going to amounts starts from the equal split in cents, and going to percentages from 100 split evenly to two decimals, the hundredths left going to the members the seed picks, in the same order as the cents of the equal split (33.34 for one of three and 33.33 for the other two), so changing from Equally to Percent keeps the extra cent on the same person for an amount up to 100.00; past it a hundredth of a percent is worth more than a cent, and the percent start is not exactly the equal split any more. The percentages are not stored: reopening an entry of that mode shows each share over the amount, to two decimals, rounded as the parts are (down, the hundredths left going to the biggest fractions) so that they add up to 100, and an entry saved without touching its fields keeps its cents, as splitting those percentages again may move a cent.
- **The payment is the payer's.** `payment` and `chargeDate` say how the **payer** paid, as for an expense of one person alone ([when an expense counts](expense-counting.md)): at once, with a credit card (counted on the day it is charged) or with a restaurant card. For every other member the expense counts as paid now, on the day it was made. Only the payer sets it: the form offers it when the payer is the person logging, and changing the payer to somebody else puts it back to `direct` with no charge date. There are no installments and no part of one's own for a shared expense, since the split is the payer's part. The server holds to it: a write whose payer isn't the caller's own member, with a card, is `422 not-the-payer`, unless the stored entry already has that card for that payer (the same payment and charge date), so anybody can still edit the rest of it. A member who records an expense for somebody else leaves it paid now, and the payer adds the card by editing it. A settlement never has one.
- **The category** is one of the default categories only, suggested from the text as for any expense ([categories](categories.md)): a custom category exists for one person, and everybody in a group has to see the same one. The server answers `400 unknown-category` to an id that isn't a default one. This is the one place where the server checks a category: default ones are never deleted, so the check can't race with a delete as it could for a custom one ([ADR-0006](../adr/0006-categories.md)).
- **Writing.** `PUT /groups/:groupId/entries/:id` is idempotent and creates or updates the entry. It applies when the caller is a member and the `updatedAt` is strictly newer than the stored one; otherwise it answers with the entry it kept. That the caller is a member who hasn't left an open group is checked again in the statement that writes, so a leave or a delete at the same moment can't let a write in. It is refused, in this order, with `404` (not a member, or no such group, the same answer for both), `409 id-taken` (the id belongs to another group, and nothing else of it is told), `409 closed` (the group is deleted, or the caller left it), `422 clock-ahead` (an `updatedAt` more than a day ahead of the server's clock, [offline sync](offline-sync.md)), `400 unknown-category`, or `422` (`kind-fixed`, below; `currency-mismatch`, `unknown-member`, or `member-left`: a new entry names a member who left, or a write moves what a member who left pays or owes in the stored entry, deleted or not, below; a placeholder merged into another member counts as that member; `not-the-payer`, a card set by somebody other than the payer, above). A write to a closed group that was sent before and landed (the stored entry has its `updatedAt`, in a version the caller reads) answers the entry instead of `409 closed`, as the routes of groups answer a write whose effect is there. That answer tells a member who left one thing past `left_seq`: replaying an entry they hold, as they hold it, they learn whether it changed after they left (`200` while it is as they hold it, `409 closed` once it was edited or deleted). It tells them nothing else, no content and no entry they don't hold, and it is accepted. A `400` is a bad shape, shares that don't add up to the amount included, as the schema refuses them. The outbox treats `400`, `403`, `404`, `409` and `422` as permanent ([offline sync](offline-sync.md)). There is no `DELETE`: deleting is the same `PUT` with `deletedAt`. A `PUT` that makes the row sends, after it answers, a push to the members it concerns ([notifications](notifications.md)).
- **Someone who left** at zero must never be given a debt or a credit by an edit. An entry that named them keeps them in the same role with the same cents as the stored entry, deleted or not: the payer stays the payer of the same amount, a share keeps its cents. Its text, day, category or card can still change; raising their share, making them the payer, leaving them out or changing the total of an equal split so that their share moves is `422 member-left`, and the person deletes the entry and enters a new one instead. Deleting such an entry, and taking the delete back with Undo, keep what it says of them and are allowed, so they can still move that person's balance ([groups](groups.md)). Once they come back they are a member like any other, and the entries that name them can be edited again.
- **Reading.** `GET /groups/:groupId/entries?after=<seq>&limit=` pages through the changes after a cursor (0 when left out), oldest first, tombstones included, and answers `{ entries, cursor, hasMore }` with the new cursor, as for the expenses (200 entries a page unless `limit` asks for fewer or more, up to 500). A person who isn't a member gets the `404` of a group that doesn't exist. `seq` is the group's own counter, taken inside the upsert (`MAX(seq) + 1` for that group), and the pair `(group_id, seq)` is unique. A member who joins reads the history from cursor 0. A member who left reads only up to the cursor the group had when they left (`left_seq`), until they come back, when they read all of it from the cursor their device holds, and the members of a deleted group read all of it ([groups](groups.md)). An entry edited or deleted after a member left takes a later `seq`, so that member never reads the change, nor the entry at all if their device hadn't pulled it before they left; no older version is kept ([offline sync](offline-sync.md)).
- **Editing and deleting** are open to every member and use the later-than rule of [expenses](expenses.md): an edit, a delete and an Undo take an `updatedAt` later than the version they replace. The row says "Added by Ana" or "Edited by Ana" when the last writer isn't the person looking. Deleting asks nothing and can be taken back for a few seconds with Undo ([ADR-0010](../adr/0010-edit-duplicate-and-delete-expenses.md)); the toast says whose group it was ("Deleted <name> from <group>"). A shared expense can't be moved to another group: it is duplicated there and deleted here. An entry keeps its kind: an expense can't become a settlement or the reverse (`422 kind-fixed`), so the person deletes it and enters a new one.
- **Duplicate** opens the form as a new shared expense in the same group, with the same payer, mode and members who are in, the day today. It is a new expense, so the cents left of an equal split are drawn again for it, not kept from the original. A settlement is not duplicated.
- **In your own expenses** (the owner's request: what is added in a group is listed in yours). `core/shared-expense.ts` turns the entries of the groups on the device into rows with the shape of an expense: the text, the category, the day, and, for everybody but a payer who used a card, **your part as the amount**, paid now. For the payer the row follows the card: a credit card expense has their part as the amount and counts on the charge date; a restaurant card expense has the total as the amount and their part as `personalCents`, so what the card paid for them is income of the month it is charged and what the others owe is derived like the money others pay back of a personal restaurant card expense. The settlement that later pays it back counts for nobody. Entries that are deleted, settlements, and expenses where your part is zero give no row (a payer with a card always has a part). The row carries what a personal one doesn't (`shared`: the group, the total and who paid), and its `id` is the entry's, so whether it is waiting to sync is read by that id from the outbox, as for a personal expense. Everything that adds up reads these rows through the helpers of `core/counting.ts` without a change, so they appear in the list, the summary, the charts, the budgets, the suggestion from the history and the CSV export ([expense list](expense-list.md), [when an expense counts](expense-counting.md), [export](export.md)); the JSON export keeps the person's own expenses as they are and holds the groups and their entries instead. Only the groups whose switch Show in my expenses is on for the person give rows ([groups](groups.md)), closed or not, and the id of a merged placeholder is read as its target. The entry is the only copy: nothing is written to the person's own expenses, and a row that leaves the group leaves the lists. A person who takes the place of a placeholder (a friend added in its place or a merge, [groups](groups.md)) takes its entries too: from then on the placeholder's parts are theirs, on the days they were made, past months included, so the summary, the charts and the budgets of those months change for them, as nothing is stored to hold them back. Their switch starts on, and turning it off takes the group out. No budget warning comes for those parts, as only an expense the person adds or duplicates warns.
- **Your member.** Your part is looked up through your member id in each group, which `GET /groups` gives as `me`.

## Screen

### The form

The add form is the one of the sheet ([expenses](expenses.md)), which the "+" opens from any screen and Edit and Duplicate of an entry of a group open too. It is split in two so the shared part doesn't turn into conditions scattered through it: the **head** (description, amount, category, day) is common, and then comes the **tail** of the choice below it.

- A `ChoiceGroup` under the head, "For", with **Just me** and **Shared**. It starts on Just me, which is what the tail of today is (the payment, the charge date, your part, the installments). From the "+" inside an open group, on its screen or its settings, it starts on Shared with that group in With, both shown and changeable; in a closed group, or anywhere else, it starts as before. Editing or duplicating an entry of a group starts on Shared with For hidden and With fixed: an entry can't change group.
- **Shared** shows, in this order, `--size-4` apart:
    - **With**, a `Select` of the groups and the friends, each with its icon (`groups`, `person`). It starts on the group of the screen when the "+" is pressed inside an open one, else on the one used last, remembered on the device for the signed-in user (`tarja:shared-with:<id>`), else on the first of the list, the groups before the friends as on the Groups tab. The form makes nobody: without any group or friend, the field is a one-line hint to make a group or invite a friend from the Groups tab ([groups](groups.md)).
    - **Split**, a `Disclosure` ([ADR-0026](../adr/0026-the-add-form-keeps-its-button-in-sight-and-folds-its-options.md)) that holds the three below. Above its toggle, which reads "Split" alone, a line in secondary text always says who paid and how it is split, and follows every change: "Paid by you · equally among 3 people", "Paid by Bea · by percent among 2 people", "Choose who paid · …" when nobody is chosen. It starts closed, except for a duplicate whose payer has left, opens and closes on a press, opens when Add finds something to fix in it (who paid, the shares, a card without you in the split), and closes again once an expense is added.
    - **Paid by**, a `Select` of the members who haven't left, starting on you. In this list, in the rows of the people and in the pickers of Settle up and Merge, the person's own member reads "Ana (You)" (`names.you`), Ana being the name the group gives that member, so a name that two members share is numbered as everywhere ("Ana (2) (You)"); a sentence keeps the plain "You" ("You paid", "You owe Bruno").
    - **How it's split**, a `ChoiceGroup`: Equally, Percent, Amounts.
    - **The people**: a row for each member who hasn't left, with a `Checkbox` and the name (yours as "Ana (You)"), and at the end the share: in Equally the amount, in plain text, and nothing for a member whose part comes to nothing; in Percent a percentage field, which ends with "%" in its box; in Amounts an amount field. A member who is out shows nothing at the end. Under the rows, a line that says what is missing: "€4.00 unassigned", "€1.50 over", "2.5% left", or nothing when it adds up. What is typed in the percentages or the amounts holds for the members who are in when it was typed (and, for the amounts, the total): changing either starts them again from the even split, as the payments of a plan do.
- **How you paid**, only when **Paid by** is you, under More options with the day ([expenses](expenses.md)): a `Select`, Now, Credit card or Restaurant card, and, for the cards, the day it is charged, proposed and validated as in a personal expense ([expenses](expenses.md)); a hint says "For the others it counts as paid now". A card needs you in the split. Installments and your part are not offered.
- For isn't offered while an expense of one person is edited: an edit doesn't turn it into a shared one.
- **Add** (or **Save** when editing) is never off ([ADR-0026](../adr/0026-the-add-form-keeps-its-button-in-sight-and-folds-its-options.md)): pressed while the amount isn't valid, nobody is in, the shares don't add up, nobody pays or a card leaves you out of the split, it saves nothing and marks each problem where it is ("€4.00 unassigned" and "2.5% left" turn from a quiet line into an error, a payer who is gone marks Paid by), with the focus on the first one. A group of one other person is the usual case and takes the same fields.
- The description and the amount stay where they are; switching For keeps them, and keeps what was typed in the other tail while the form is open. Adding an expense puts For back to its start. A custom category picked in Just me is replaced, when switching to Shared, by the suggestion from the text (Other when there is none), and a hint under the field says "Custom categories aren't available in shared expenses"; the list of categories in Shared holds the default ones only.
- Edit, Duplicate and Delete from the list, or from the ledger of a group, work as for any expense ([expenses](expenses.md)): the sheet opens with For on Shared, hidden, and With fixed to that group; the title reads "Edit the expense" or "Duplicate the expense". The form starts from the entry (`draftOfEntry`): its payer, its mode, who is in (a merged placeholder read as its target), the percentages as above and the amounts as saved; while neither the amount nor anything of the split is touched an edit saves the cents it was opened with (a duplicate has no saved cents: its cents left are drawn like those of a new expense), and Save writes the same id and `createdAt` with an `updatedAt` later than the version it replaces. How you paid holds the card only when you paid; when somebody else did, an edit keeps their card and charge date (unless the payer changes, which puts it back to now), and a duplicate is paid now. Save, Add or Cancel put the form back to adding, empty, with the focus on the row (the one copied from), or on the title of the ledger when it is gone; deleting the entry being edited puts it back to adding too. Closing the sheet ends the edit or the copy as Cancel does.
- The device doesn't offer Edit for an entry that names somebody who left (as payer or in a share, a merged placeholder read as its target): the button is off and a quiet notice under it says to delete it and add it again, as most edits of it are `422 member-left`. Duplicate leaves that person out, and when they paid, Paid by starts on nobody, says so in a hint, and Add marks it until somebody who is in the group is chosen, so the copy is never paid by someone the person didn't pick. Delete and Undo stay.
- A budget warning can come when an expense is added or duplicated, for your part ([budgets](budgets.md)).

### The ledger

The entries of a group on the screen of the group, after the balances and titled "Expenses". Under the title a `ChoiceGroup` named "Order" holds two ways of reading the ledger, there once the group has an entry, and the start is always **Recently added**: the choice lives in the screen, so it is not remembered between visits, and it survives the ledger being refreshed.

- **Recently added**: one flat list of the entries that aren't deleted, the latest made first (`createdAt`, then the id, so the order is stable), on a single `Sheet` with no label and no total. An edit never moves an entry, so an expense dated three months ago and just added is at the top. With no day over them, the rows say their own, first in their details: the short date in the language of the app, with the year unless it is this year ("Oct 25", "Dec 31, 2025").
- **By date**: the newest day first and, within a day, the latest made first, a day on each `Sheet` as the expenses are ([the ticket](../../design-system/DESIGN.md#3-shape)): the long weekday date as the label (with the year when the ledger goes over a New Year), and at the foot the total of the day's expenses, which a day of settlements only doesn't have, as nothing was spent. The rows don't repeat the date.

The order is only how the ledger of the screen reads: the add form's hints, the totals, the balances and the export don't change with it. Deleted entries aren't there, and with no entry a quiet `Notice` says "No expenses yet." instead of the choice. Show more and the focus rules below work the same in both, over the rows in the order in use. A row is a `Disclosure`:

- An **expense**: the badge of its category, the name (the text, else the category), the total as the amount, and in the details who paid ("Paid by Ana", "You paid") and your part ("Your part €25.00", or "Not in this one" when you are out). Written last by someone else, it says "Added by Ana" while it was never edited (`updatedAt` is `createdAt`) and "Edited by Ana" after, "Deleted user" for a person who deleted their account; not synced yet, it says so.
- A **settlement**: "Ana paid Bruno", "You paid Ana", "Ana paid you", with the amount, and no category; who wrote it and whether it is synced, as for an expense.
- The panel holds Edit, Duplicate (not for a settlement) and Delete as ghost buttons `--size-4` apart. Edit loads an expense into the add form and a settlement into Settle up ([balances](balances.md)). Show more adds 100 rows at a time and puts the focus on the first row it brings in.
- Delete works as in the list of expenses ([expenses](expenses.md)): the row fades out, the focus goes at once to the next row, else to the one before, else to the title of the ledger, and once the delete is safe on the device the toast says "Deleted Dinner from Trip" ("Deleted the settlement “You paid Bea” from Trip") with Undo, which brings the entry back with the focus on its row. A delete or an Undo the device refuses (the group closed meanwhile) is said under the title, and the row of a refused delete fades out and comes back.
- In a closed group the rows have nothing to open: no chevron and no panel.

### What the server refused

A write the server refused for good ([offline sync](offline-sync.md)) is said in a `Notice` with `role="alert"` and a Dismiss beside it, one each: "A change to “Dinner” in Trip was discarded." and why, in words, for `closed`, `member-left`, `unknown-member`, `not-the-payer`, `kind-fixed`, `currency-mismatch`, `unknown-category`, `id-taken` and `clock-ahead` (the clock of the device is ahead: check its date and time), and "The server refused it." for any other. A settlement is named by who paid whom. They show at home under the sync status and on the Groups tab, all of them, and on the screen of a group under its title, those of that group. They live in memory only, so a reload forgets them.

### In your expenses

- A shared row in the list ([expense list](expense-list.md)) shows your part as the amount, like a restaurant card shows yours, and says in the details the group (or the person, for a friend), who paid and the total when it isn't your part: "Trip to Lisbon · Ana paid €100.00". Its panel holds the same three buttons and they act on the entry of the group.
