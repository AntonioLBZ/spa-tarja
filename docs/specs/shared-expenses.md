# Shared expenses

An entry of a group is an expense split between its members, or a settlement ([balances](balances.md)). The decisions are in [ADR-0012](../adr/0012-shared-expenses.md) and [ADR-0013](../adr/0013-shared-entries-sync-through-an-outbox.md).

## Rules

- **The entry.** `id` (a UUID made on the client, read in lower case like the ids of groups and members), `kind` (`expense` or `settlement`), `text` (up to 200 characters), `categoryId` (an expense only), `amountCents` (1 to `MAX_AMOUNT_CENTS`), `currency` (the group's), `date` (`YYYY-MM-DD`), `paidBy` (a member id), `split` (`equal`, `percent` or `exact`), `shares` (a list of `{ memberId, cents }`, at most 100, `MAX_SHARES`: more than a group's members can be over time, those who left and merged included), `payment` (`direct`, the default, `credit` or `voucher`) and `chargeDate`, `createdAt`, `updatedAt` and `deletedAt` (epoch milliseconds, safe integers, so they are stored exactly). The server records who made it and who wrote it last (`created_by` and `updated_by`, user ids from the session, never from the client) and its `seq`; the answers carry those two people as their members in the group (`createdBy` and `updatedBy`, null once that person deleted their account), never a user id. The shape is validated in `core/` by one schema the client and the server share (`core/shared-entry.ts`), with these checks: the shares name each member once, each has at least one cent, they add up to `amountCents` exactly, a settlement has no category, has a single share, is not paid to oneself, is paid `direct` and is split `exact` (its one share is the amount), a `credit` or `voucher` expense has a `chargeDate` not before `date` and a share of the payer of at least one cent, and a `direct` one has no `chargeDate` (a shared expense has no installments). A `payment` left out is `direct` and a `chargeDate` left out is none. The schema checks only the shape of the category; that it is a default one is checked apart, with `isDefaultCategoryId` (`core/categories.ts`, the list the default categories are defined in), so the server can answer `unknown-category` (below).
- **Who paid** is one member who hasn't left; the person who is logging it by default, but anybody can record that another member paid. Several payers are left for later; they would be a new nullable column that overrides `paidBy`.
- **Splitting** is done on the device, in cents, in `core/split.ts`, and what it works out is stored as `shares`; the mode is stored only so the form opens as it was left.
    - **Equally** among the members who are in (everybody, at the start): each gets the amount divided, and the remainder goes one cent at a time to the first ones in the order the group lists its members (when they were added, then by id), as the first payments of a plan do ([ADR-0004](../adr/0004-data-conventions.md)). A member whose part comes to nothing (an amount of fewer cents than the members who are in) is left out of the shares, since a share is at least one cent.
    - **By percent**: each member who is in takes a percentage with up to two decimals, 0 to 100, which must add up to 100. A part is the amount times the percentage, rounded down; the cents that are left go one at a time to the biggest fractions, and on a tie to the first in the group's order. A member at 0 is left out, and so is one whose part comes to nothing. The device works in hundredths of a percent, whole numbers as the cents are (`HUNDRED_PERCENT` is 10 000).
    - **By amount**: each member who is in takes an exact amount. The form shows what is still unassigned and refuses to save until it is zero, and refuses a part that goes over the total, like the installments.
    - **Leaving someone out** is unchecking them: their share is zero and they aren't in the list of shares. At least one member has to be in. The payer can be left out (paying for the others).
    - Changing the mode keeps who is in. Going to amounts starts from the equal split in cents, and going to percentages from 100 split evenly to two decimals, the hundredths left going to the first ones (33.34, 33.33 and 33.33 for three). The percentages are not stored: reopening an entry of that mode shows each share over the amount, to two decimals, rounded as the parts are (down, the hundredths left going to the biggest fractions) so that they add up to 100, and an entry saved without touching its fields keeps its cents, as splitting those percentages again may move a cent.
- **The payment is the payer's.** `payment` and `chargeDate` say how the **payer** paid, as for an expense of one person alone ([when an expense counts](expense-counting.md)): at once, with a credit card (counted on the day it is charged) or with a restaurant card. For every other member the expense counts as paid now, on the day it was made. Only the payer sets it: the form offers it when the payer is the person logging, and changing the payer to somebody else puts it back to `direct` with no charge date. There are no installments and no part of one's own for a shared expense, since the split is the payer's part. The server checks only the shape.
- **The category** is one of the default categories only, suggested from the text as for any expense ([categories](categories.md)): a custom category exists for one person, and everybody in a group has to see the same one. The server answers `400 unknown-category` to an id that isn't a default one. This is the one place where the server checks a category: default ones are never deleted, so the check can't race with a delete as it could for a custom one ([ADR-0006](../adr/0006-categories.md)).
- **Writing.** `PUT /groups/:groupId/entries/:id` is idempotent and creates or updates the entry. It applies when the caller is a member and the `updatedAt` is strictly newer than the stored one; otherwise it answers with the entry it kept. That the caller is a member who hasn't left an open group is checked again in the statement that writes, so a leave or a delete at the same moment can't let a write in. It is refused, in this order, with `404` (not a member, or no such group, the same answer for both), `409 id-taken` (the id belongs to another group, and nothing else of it is told), `409 closed` (the group is deleted, or the caller left it), `422 clock-ahead` (an `updatedAt` more than a day ahead of the server's clock, [offline sync](offline-sync.md)), `400 unknown-category`, or `422` (`currency-mismatch`, `unknown-member`, or `member-left`: a new entry names a member who left, or a write moves what a member who left pays or owes in the stored entry, deleted or not, below; a placeholder merged into another member counts as that member). A write to a closed group that was sent before and landed (the stored entry has its `updatedAt`, in a version the caller reads) answers the entry instead of `409 closed`, as the routes of groups answer a write whose effect is there. A `400` is a bad shape, shares that don't add up to the amount included, as the schema refuses them. The outbox treats `400`, `403`, `404`, `409` and `422` as permanent ([offline sync](offline-sync.md)). There is no `DELETE`: deleting is the same `PUT` with `deletedAt`.
- **Someone who left** at zero must never be given a debt or a credit by an edit. An entry that named them keeps them in the same role with the same cents as the stored entry, deleted or not: the payer stays the payer of the same amount, a share keeps its cents. Its text, day, category or card can still change; raising their share, making them the payer, leaving them out or changing the total of an equal split so that their share moves is `422 member-left`, and the person deletes the entry and enters a new one instead. Deleting such an entry, and taking the delete back with Undo, keep what it says of them and are allowed, so they can still move that person's balance ([groups](groups.md)).
- **Reading.** `GET /groups/:groupId/entries?after=<seq>&limit=` pages through the changes after a cursor (0 when left out), oldest first, tombstones included, and answers `{ entries, cursor, hasMore }` with the new cursor, as for the expenses (200 entries a page unless `limit` asks for fewer or more, up to 500). A person who isn't a member gets the `404` of a group that doesn't exist. `seq` is the group's own counter, taken inside the upsert (`MAX(seq) + 1` for that group), and the pair `(group_id, seq)` is unique. A member who joins reads the history from cursor 0. A member who left reads only up to the cursor the group had when they left (`left_seq`), and the members of a deleted group read all of it ([groups](groups.md)). An entry edited or deleted after a member left takes a later `seq`, so that member never reads the change, nor the entry at all if their device hadn't pulled it before they left; no older version is kept ([offline sync](offline-sync.md)).
- **Editing and deleting** are open to every member and use the later-than rule of [expenses](expenses.md): an edit, a delete and an Undo take an `updatedAt` later than the version they replace. The row says "Added by Ana" or "Edited by Ana" when the last writer isn't the person looking. Deleting asks nothing and can be taken back for a few seconds with Undo ([ADR-0010](../adr/0010-edit-duplicate-and-delete-expenses.md)); the toast says whose group it was ("Deleted <name> from <group>"). A shared expense can't be moved to another group: it is duplicated there and deleted here.
- **Duplicate** opens the form as a new shared expense in the same group, with the same payer, mode and members who are in, the day today. A settlement is not duplicated.
- **In your own expenses** (the owner's request: what is added in a group is listed in yours). `core/shared-expense.ts` turns the entries of the groups on the device into rows with the shape of an expense: the text, the category, the day, and, for everybody but a payer who used a card, **your part as the amount**, paid now. For the payer the row follows the card: a credit card expense has their part as the amount and counts on the charge date; a restaurant card expense has the total as the amount and their part as `personalCents`, so what the card paid for them is income of the month it is charged and what the others owe is derived like the money others pay back of a personal restaurant card expense. The settlement that later pays it back counts for nobody. Entries that are deleted, settlements, and expenses where your part is zero give no row (a payer with a card always has a part). The row carries what a personal one doesn't (`shared`: the group, the total and who paid), and its `id` is the entry's, so whether it is waiting to sync is read by that id from the outbox, as for a personal expense. Everything that adds up reads these rows through the helpers of `core/counting.ts` without a change, so they appear in the list, the summary, the charts, the budgets, the suggestion from the history and the exports ([expense list](expense-list.md), [when an expense counts](expense-counting.md)). Only the groups whose switch Show in my expenses is on for the person give rows ([groups](groups.md)), closed or not, and the id of a merged placeholder is read as its target. The entry is the only copy: nothing is written to the person's own expenses, and a row that leaves the group leaves the lists.
- **Your member.** Your part is looked up through your member id in each group, which `GET /groups` gives as `me`.

## Screen

### The form

The add form is the one at home ([expenses](expenses.md)), and it is also the one that heads a group. It is split in two so the shared part doesn't turn into conditions scattered through it: the **head** (description, amount, category, day) is common, and then comes the **tail** of the choice below it.

- A `ChoiceGroup` under the head, "For", with **Just me** and **Shared**. At home it starts on Just me, which is what the tail of today is (the payment, the charge date, your part, the installments). In a group it starts on Shared and is hidden: the group is already chosen.
- **Shared** shows, in this order, `--size-4` apart:
    - **With**, a `Select` of the groups and the friends, each with its icon (`groups`, `person`), and a last option "New person…" that asks for a name and makes a friend on the spot. It starts on the one used last, remembered on the device (`tarja:shared-with`), and on the only one when there is one. Without any, the field is a hint and the "New person…" form. "New person…" needs a connection ([offline sync](offline-sync.md)); offline it is off with a hint, and the groups and friends the device holds work.
    - **Paid by**, a `Select` of the members who haven't left, starting on you.
    - **Split**, a `ChoiceGroup`: Equally, Percent, Amounts.
    - **The people**: a row for each member who hasn't left, with a `Checkbox` and the name, and at the end the share: in Equally the amount, in plain text; in Percent a percentage field; in Amounts an amount field. A member who is out shows nothing at the end. Under the rows, a line that says what is missing: "€4.00 unassigned", "€1.50 over", "2.5 % left", or nothing when it adds up.
- **How you paid**, only when **Paid by** is you: a `Select`, Now, Credit card or Restaurant card, and, for the cards, the day it is charged, proposed and validated as in a personal expense ([expenses](expenses.md)); a hint says "For the others it counts as paid now". A card needs you in the split. Installments and your part are not offered.
- **Add** (or **Save** when editing) is off until the amount is valid, at least one member is in and the shares add up. A group of one other person is the usual case and takes the same fields.
- The description and the amount stay where they are; switching For keeps them, and keeps what was typed in the other tail while the form is open. Adding an expense puts For back to its start. A custom category picked in Just me is replaced, when switching to Shared, by the suggestion from the text (Other when there is none), and a hint under the field says "Custom categories aren't available in shared expenses"; the list of categories in Shared holds the default ones only.
- Edit, Duplicate and Delete from the list, or from the ledger of a group, work as for any expense ([expenses](expenses.md)): the form opens at home, or in the group when it is there, with For on Shared and With fixed to that group; the heading reads "Edit the expense" or "Duplicate the expense".
- A budget warning can come when an expense is added or duplicated, for your part ([budgets](budgets.md)).

### The ledger

The entries of a group on the screen of the group, newest first by day, on `Sheet`s as the expenses are ([the ticket](../../design-system/DESIGN.md#3-shape)), with the total of the day's expenses at the foot. A row is a `Disclosure`:

- An **expense**: the badge of its category, the name (the text, else the category), the total as the amount, and in the details who paid ("Paid by Ana", "You paid") and your part ("Your part €25.00", or "Not in this one" when you are out). Edited by someone else, or not synced yet, it says so.
- A **settlement**: "Ana paid Bruno", "You paid Ana", "Ana paid you", with the amount, and no category.
- The panel holds Edit, Duplicate (not for a settlement) and Delete as ghost buttons `--size-4` apart. Show more adds 100 rows at a time.

### In your expenses

- A shared row in the list ([expense list](expense-list.md)) shows your part as the amount, like a restaurant card shows yours, and says in the details the group (or the person, for a friend), who paid and the total when it isn't your part: "Trip to Lisbon · Ana paid €100.00". Its panel holds the same three buttons and they act on the entry of the group.
