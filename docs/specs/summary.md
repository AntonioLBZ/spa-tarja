# Summary

## The hero (home)

The home opens on `MonthHero`, a `HeroStat` for the current month: "Spent in {month}" over what the month has cost so far (counts as the summary does, [when an expense counts](expense-counting.md)), and under it one line: "{left} left of {income}" or "{over} more than you earned" when the income is set, "of your {limit} limit · {left} left" or "{over} over your {limit} limit" when a total budget is, and a link to the Summary to set the income when neither is. Under the lines stands **the comb** of the month: one straight bar of ink per day (`MonthComb`, weeks apart by a longer gap, starting on the weekday the month does), as tall as what was spent that day (at least a stub; a day with nothing is a stub in secondary text, the days to come a fainter one), today with a flag in the accent color over its bar, and the first, the middle and the last day named under it. A pointer moving along it, or a finger moving sideways (the page still scrolls up and down), reads the nearest bar's day into the line under the figure ("Sat, 9 Nov: €10.00"), and a tap takes the list to that day. It is hidden from assistive technology, since the list says all of it.

## The Summary screen

- `/summary`, linked from the header, shows one month at a time. It opens on the current month, whose name sits between two arrow buttons (previous and next, at least `--size-touch` wide); the month isn't in the URL.
- **The hero.** Under the month switch, a `HeroStat` says the answer: "Left to spend" with what is left of the income when one is set, with "of {income} income" under it, and "Spent so far" with what the month has cost, and the hint that the income sets what is left, when none is. The figure counts up. The rows below stay, as the breakdown.
- The month's numbers are notch-bordered rows: income (what the person set, or "Not set"), restaurant card (what the card paid for you of the expenses charged in the month, only when there is some), paid back by others (what others paid back of the restaurant card expenses made in the month, only when there is some), spent, and left. Left is the row that matters: bold, and in `--color-danger` below zero. It stays hidden, with a hint, until the income is set. The rows are `--size-2` apart, as in the category list, and the arrows of the month and the income field with its buttons are `--size-4` apart, also when the income row wraps.
- Under the rows, one form sets the income: an amount field and Save, and Remove once there is one. Setting it needs a connection ([when an expense counts](expense-counting.md)), so offline the form is disabled, the field as well as its buttons, and says why; the numbers themselves still show from the local copy.
- Under the numbers come the bars of the limits ([budgets](budgets.md)). Two links at the bottom lead to the budgets and to the charts, `--size-4` apart.
- The numbers include your part of the shared expenses ([when an expense counts](expense-counting.md)). A settlement is not in them.
