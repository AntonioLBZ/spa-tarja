# Summary

- `/summary`, linked from the header, shows one month at a time. It opens on the current month, whose name sits in the field between two circular arrow buttons (previous and next, at least `--size-touch` wide); the month isn't in the URL. Under it the **hero**: what is left to spend of the month in the biggest type of the screen once the income is set ("Left to spend", with "of <income> income" under it), and what was spent so far until then ("Spent so far", with the hint to set the income). The number counts up when it changes. While the sheet covers the hero, the bar shows the month and the number.
- The month's numbers are rows on a tinted surface: income (what the person set, or "Not set"), restaurant card (what the card paid for you of the expenses charged in the month, only when there is some), paid back by others (what others paid back of the restaurant card expenses made in the month, only when there is some), spent, and left. Left is the row that matters: bold, and in `--color-danger` below zero. It stays hidden until the income is set; the hero says why. The rows are `--size-2` apart, and the income field with its buttons `--size-2` apart, also when the income row wraps.
- Under the rows, one form sets the income: an amount field and Save, and Remove once there is one. Setting it needs a connection ([when an expense counts](expense-counting.md)), so offline the form is disabled, the field as well as its buttons, and says why; the numbers themselves still show from the local copy.
- Under the numbers come the bars of the limits ([budgets](budgets.md)). Two links at the bottom, buttons with a chevron, lead to the budgets and to the charts.
- The numbers include your part of the shared expenses ([when an expense counts](expense-counting.md)). A settlement is not in them.
