# Groups

The decision is in [ADR-0012](../adr/0012-shared-expenses.md). What the entries hold and how they are split is in [shared expenses](shared-expenses.md), the debts in [balances](balances.md), and how people come in, are merged and leave the app in [invitations](invitations.md) and [ADR-0014](../adr/0014-people-in-groups.md).

## Rules

- **Kinds.** A `group` has a name and any number of members up to the limit. A `direct` group is a friend: two members, no name, no way to add a third. Everything below holds for both unless it says otherwise. A group is made with the app's currency (`DEFAULT_CURRENCY`), stored on it and on every entry.
- **Members.** A member has an `id` (a UUID made on the client, which the entries refer to), a `name`, a `role` (`owner` or `member`) and, once somebody has claimed it, a user. One that nobody has claimed is a **placeholder**, the dummy. The name is the one the group uses for that person: whoever makes a placeholder chooses it, the owner can rename any member, and a user can rename themselves. A user starts with the name of their Google account. The username and the email never appear in a group ([invitations](invitations.md)). Two members whose names are the same, ignoring case, are told apart on the screen by a number after the name ("Ana", "Ana (2)", in the order the group lists them); the stored name is untouched. A member whose name is empty is a person who deleted their account, and the screens call them "Deleted user".
- **Making a group.** The maker becomes the `owner` and a member named after their Google account, and can add the others at once as placeholders, by name. A friend is made by name too: one placeholder next to the maker. Both can be invited afterwards ([invitations](invitations.md)). Making one needs a connection ([offline sync](offline-sync.md)).
- **Who can do what.** Any member adds placeholders, invites, and adds, edits and deletes entries. Only the owner renames the group, removes other members, merges a placeholder, switches the simplified debts, hands the group over and deletes it. In a friend, either member can do what the owner does, as there is nothing to hand over or to remove. The server decides on every call; the screen only hides what a person can't do.
- **Merging a dummy.** A placeholder can become a real person that is already in the group: the owner merges it **into a member who is a user**, and only that (two users, or two placeholders, are never merged). The placeholder row stays with `merged_into` set and `left_at` set, and from then on every id of it, in the old entries and in the ones written offline before the merge, means the target, in the balances, the ledger and the lists (`canonicalMember` in `core/`). Nothing in the entries is rewritten, so nothing has to be synced again and an offline write never breaks. A merge can't be undone. An open invitation of the placeholder is revoked.
- **Leaving and removing.** A member leaves, or the owner removes one, only while that member's balance is zero ([balances](balances.md)); otherwise the answer is `409 unsettled`, and the screen says to settle up first. The member row stays with `left_at` set, so the entries keep naming that person; a placeholder removed the same way. The owner can't leave: they hand the group over to another member who is a user, or delete it. Removing a user also revokes their open invitation. The balance check is not atomic with a write that another device has not synced yet; a late entry can leave a balance on someone who has left, and it shows in the balances as that person's name with "(left)".
- **Deleting a group.** Only the owner, and only when every balance is zero, or when no member but the owner is a user (a group of placeholders hurts nobody). Otherwise `409 unsettled`. The group gets `deleted_at` and is **closed**; nothing else is deleted.
- **Closed groups.** A group is closed for a person when it was deleted, or when they left or were removed. Deleting a group does not delete the expenses: a closed group stays on the person's devices and in their `GET /groups`, read-only. A group that was deleted keeps all its entries readable by its members; one a person left keeps what the group held at the moment they left (`left_seq`), so they never see what the group spends afterwards. Every write to a closed group answers `409 closed`. A closed group does not count toward the limit of 30, can't be settled or edited, and its balances stay as they were.
- **Show in my expenses.** Each member has a switch per group, on by default, `show_in_expenses`. While it is on, the person's parts of that group's expenses are in their own expenses: in the list, the summary, the charts and the budgets ([when an expense counts](expense-counting.md)). While it is off none of them is, and the group is still there to open. It holds for open and closed groups alike, so turning it off is how a person leaves a closed group out of their expenses. It belongs to the person and is kept on the server, so every device of the person adds up the same; changing it needs a connection.
- **Limits**: 30 open groups per user, 30 members per group (those who haven't left), a name of 1 to 40 characters.
- **API.** All of it needs a session ([authentication](authentication.md)); a group the caller is not in, and has never been in, answers `404`, so its existence isn't told.
    - `GET /groups` answers the groups the caller is in, closed ones too: `id`, `kind`, `name`, `currency`, `simplifyDebts`, `closed` (`null`, `deleted` or `left`), `me` (the caller's member id), `showInExpenses` and `members` (`id`, `name`, `role`, `claimed`, `leftAt`, `mergedInto`, the left ones included so old entries can name them), never a user id.
    - `PUT /groups/:id` is idempotent. When the group doesn't exist it makes it, with `{ kind, name, currency, me: { id, name }, others: [{ id, name }] }`; when it does, only the owner, and only `{ name, simplifyDebts }` change. An id that belongs to a group the caller is not in is `409 id-taken`.
    - `DELETE /groups/:id` deletes it as above.
    - `PUT /groups/:groupId/members/:memberId` with `{ name }` makes a placeholder or renames a member. `DELETE` on it leaves or removes. `POST /groups/:groupId/members/:memberId/merge` with `{ into }` merges. `POST /groups/:groupId/owner` with `{ memberId }` hands the group over.
    - `PUT /groups/:groupId/me` with `{ showInExpenses }` sets the switch of the caller.
    - The entries and the invitations have their routes in [shared expenses](shared-expenses.md) and [invitations](invitations.md).

## Screen

- **Groups** (`/groups`, depth 1, a tab of the bottom bar and a word of the header: [bottom navigation](bottom-navigation.md)). From the top: the totals across your open groups, as `Notice`s in words, "You are owed €35.00" and "You owe €12.50" (each only when it isn't zero; "All settled" when both are zero); the invitations waiting for you, each a `Card` with the group, who invited you and Join and Decline as ghost buttons; two `block` buttons, New group and Add a friend, each opening its form under it as a `Disclosure`; then the groups on a `Sheet` labeled "Groups", and the friends on another labeled "Friends". Each row is the name, the balance in words ("You owe €12.50", "Ana owes you €8.00", "Settled") and a chevron, and leads to the group. Rows are `--size-2` apart as in the category list. Without any group, a quiet `Notice` says so over the two buttons. At the end, a `Disclosure` closed by default, "Closed groups", lists the closed ones on a `Sheet` of its own, each with the word "Deleted" or "You left" and whether it shows in your expenses; it is left out when there are none.
- The form of **New group** has the name and, under it, a field to add people by name, one at a time, listed with a remove button each; the main button makes the group and opens it. The form of **Add a friend** has the name of the person and, optionally, their username or email to invite them at once; it makes the friend and opens it.
- **A group** (`/groups/$groupId`, depth 2): the title is the name (the other person's name for a friend); then the balance in words, the balances and the settle up form ([balances](balances.md)), the add form, collapsed under a heading like the one at home, and the ledger of entries ([shared expenses](shared-expenses.md)). A ghost link at the end, "Edit group" ("Edit friend"), leads to its settings. A closed group has a quiet `Notice` instead of the forms ("This group was deleted on 12 Nov. Its expenses stay as they were."), the balances and the ledger without any action, and only the link to its settings.
- **Edit a group** (`/groups/$groupId/edit`, depth 3): sections `--size-8` apart.
    - **Show in my expenses**, a `Checkbox` for everybody, with the hint "When it's off, this group's expenses are not in your list, summary, charts or budgets." It is the only section of a closed group.
    - **Name**, for the owner, and the switch of the simplified debts, a `Checkbox` with a hint ([balances](balances.md)).
    - **People**: a `Sheet` with a row for each member, name first, "You" for the caller, "Owner" for the owner and "Not joined" for a placeholder, with the balance in words. A row opens (a `Disclosure` like the expenses) with its actions as ghost buttons `--size-4` apart: Rename; Invite (a placeholder), which leads to the invitation ([invitations](invitations.md)); Merge into… (a placeholder, for the owner, when the group has a member who is a user), which asks in the panel with a `Select` of those members and says it can't be undone; Make owner (a user, for the owner); Remove (for the owner, not on themselves), which asks in the panel as the categories do. Under the sheet, a field and a button add a person by name.
    - **Leave** (everybody but the owner) and **Delete** (the owner), each a `danger` `Button` that asks in a panel: the question, what happens ("The group closes. The expenses stay in your list, and you can turn them off here."), and Confirm and Cancel as ghost buttons, with the focus on Cancel. When a balance isn't zero the panel says so instead and offers the link to settle up.
- **Offline.** The lists and the groups open from the local copy and so do the entries, with the add form. Everything that shapes a group (the forms, the buttons of the panels, the switches, including Show in my expenses) is off and a `Notice` at the top says why, as in the categories ([offline sync](offline-sync.md)).
- A group the device has never held that is gone answers with a `Notice` and a link back to Groups.
