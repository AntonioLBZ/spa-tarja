# Groups

The decision is in [ADR-0012](../adr/0012-shared-expenses.md). What the entries hold and how they are split is in [shared expenses](shared-expenses.md), the debts in [balances](balances.md), and how people come in in [invitations](invitations.md).

## Rules

- **Kinds.** A `group` has a name and any number of members up to the limit. A `direct` group is a friend: two members, no name, no way to add a third. Everything below holds for both unless it says otherwise. A group is made with the app's currency (`DEFAULT_CURRENCY`), stored on it and on every entry.
- **Members.** A member has an `id` (a UUID made on the client, which the entries refer to), a `name`, a `role` (`owner` or `member`) and, once somebody has claimed it, a user. One that nobody has claimed is a **placeholder**. The name is the one the group uses for that person: whoever makes a placeholder chooses it, the owner can rename any member, and a user can rename themselves. A user starts with the name of their Google account. The username and the email never appear in a group ([invitations](invitations.md)).
- **Making a group.** The maker becomes the `owner` and a member named after their Google account, and can add the others at once as placeholders, by name. A friend is made by name too: one placeholder next to the maker. Both can be invited afterwards ([invitations](invitations.md)). Making one needs a connection ([offline sync](offline-sync.md)).
- **Who can do what.** Any member adds placeholders, invites, and adds, edits and deletes entries. Only the owner renames the group, removes other members, switches the simplified debts, hands the group over and deletes it. In a friend, either member can do what the owner does, as there is nothing to hand over or to remove. The server decides on every call; the screen only hides what a person can't do.
- **Leaving and removing.** A member leaves, or the owner removes one, only while that member's balance is zero ([balances](balances.md)); otherwise the answer is `409 unsettled`, and the screen says to settle up first. The member row stays with `left_at` set, so the entries keep naming that person; a placeholder removed the same way. The owner can't leave: they hand the group over to another member who is a user, or delete it. Removing a user also revokes their open invitation. The balance check is not atomic with a write that another device has not synced yet; a late entry can leave a balance on someone who has left, and it shows in the balances as that person's name with "(left)".
- **Deleting a group.** Only the owner, and only when every balance is zero, or when no member but the owner is a user (a group of placeholders hurts nobody). Otherwise `409 unsettled`. The group gets `deleted_at` and its entries stay in D1 but are never read again. The group leaves the answer of `GET /groups` and every device finds out the next time it asks.
- **Limits**: 30 groups per user, 30 members per group (those who haven't left), a name of 1 to 40 characters.
- **When a group leaves a device** (the person left, was removed, or the group was deleted), the entries it held are what the person's parts were made of, and dropping them would change the totals of past months ([when an expense counts](expense-counting.md)). So the app asks first: a pinned `Toast` says "<Group> is gone. Keep your N expenses from it as your own?" with Keep and Not now as ghost `Button`s. **Keep** writes an ordinary expense for each shared expense in which the person's part was more than zero: the same text, category and day, the part as the amount, paid at once, through the outbox of expenses with new ids. **Not now** drops the entries and their parts leave the totals. Until the person answers, the group stays in the local copy, marked as gone, and its parts keep counting. When the person leaves or deletes the group themselves, the same question is asked in the confirmation, before anything is sent. A device that never held the entries has nothing to offer.
- **API.** All of it needs a session ([authentication](authentication.md)); a group the caller is not in answers `404`, so its existence isn't told.
    - `GET /groups` answers the groups the caller is in (not left, not deleted): `id`, `kind`, `name`, `currency`, `simplifyDebts`, `me` (the caller's member id) and `members` (`id`, `name`, `role`, `claimed`, `leftAt`, the left ones included so old entries can name them), never a user id.
    - `PUT /groups/:id` is idempotent. When the group doesn't exist it makes it, with `{ kind, name, currency, me: { id, name }, others: [{ id, name }] }`; when it does, only the owner, and only `{ name, simplifyDebts }` change. An id that belongs to a group the caller is not in is `409 id-taken`.
    - `DELETE /groups/:id` deletes it as above.
    - `PUT /groups/:groupId/members/:memberId` with `{ name }` makes a placeholder or renames a member. `DELETE` on it leaves or removes. `POST /groups/:groupId/owner` with `{ memberId }` hands the group over.
    - The entries and the invitations have their routes in [shared expenses](shared-expenses.md) and [invitations](invitations.md).

## Screen

- **Groups** (`/groups`, depth 1, a tab of the bottom bar and a word of the header: [bottom navigation](bottom-navigation.md)). From the top: the totals across your groups, as `Notice`s in words, "You are owed €35.00" and "You owe €12.50" (each only when it isn't zero; "All settled" when both are zero); the invitations waiting for you, each a `Card` with the group, who invited you and Join and Decline as ghost buttons; two `block` buttons, New group and Add a friend, each opening its form under it as a `Disclosure`; then the groups on a `Sheet` labeled "Groups", and the friends on another labeled "Friends". Each row is the name, the balance in words ("You owe €12.50", "Ana owes you €8.00", "Settled") and a chevron, and leads to the group. Rows are `--size-2` apart as in the category list. Without any group, a quiet `Notice` says so over the two buttons.
- The form of **New group** has the name and, under it, a field to add people by name, one at a time, listed with a remove button each; the main button makes the group and opens it. The form of **Add a friend** has the name of the person and, optionally, their username or email to invite them at once; it makes the friend and opens it.
- **A group** (`/groups/$groupId`, depth 2): the title is the name (the other person's name for a friend); then the balance in words, the balances and the settle up form ([balances](balances.md)), the add form, collapsed under a heading like the one at home, and the ledger of entries ([shared expenses](shared-expenses.md)). A ghost link at the end, "Edit group" ("Edit friend"), leads to its settings.
- **Edit a group** (`/groups/$groupId/edit`, depth 3): sections `--size-8` apart.
    - **Name**, for the owner, and the switch of the simplified debts, a `Checkbox` with a hint ([balances](balances.md)).
    - **People**: a `Sheet` with a row for each member, name first, "You" for the caller, "Owner" for the owner and "Not joined" for a placeholder, with the balance in words. A row opens (a `Disclosure` like the expenses) with its actions as ghost buttons `--size-4` apart: Rename; Invite (a placeholder), which leads to the invitation ([invitations](invitations.md)); Make owner (a user, for the owner); Remove (for the owner, not on themselves), which asks in the panel as the categories do. Under the sheet, a field and a button add a person by name.
    - **Leave** (everybody but the owner) and **Delete** (the owner), each a `danger` `Button` that asks in a panel: the question, what happens, the question of keeping your part, and Confirm and Cancel as ghost buttons, with the focus on Cancel. When a balance isn't zero the panel says so instead and offers the link to settle up.
- **Offline.** The lists and the groups open from the local copy and so do the entries, with the add form. Everything that shapes a group (the forms, the buttons of the panels, the switches) is off and a `Notice` at the top says why, as in the categories ([offline sync](offline-sync.md)).
- A group that is gone (deleted, or the person left) answers with a `Notice` and a link back to Groups; if the device still holds it, the question of keeping the parts comes first.
