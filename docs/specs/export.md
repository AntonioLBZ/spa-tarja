# Exporting your data

Settings offers a JSON file with all your data and a CSV file with the expenses (export only, no import for now: [ADR-0001](../adr/0001-scope-of-the-app.md)). Both are built on the device from what it already holds, the persisted cache with the outbox on top (`applyPending`), so they work offline and send nothing to the server. Settings reads the expenses, the categories, the income and the budgets like any other screen does, so a device that never opened the summary still has its income and its budgets in the JSON; a device that has never heard from the server exports only what is waiting in its outbox. Deleted expenses are left out, and the logic is pure, in `core/export.ts`.

- **JSON** is all your data: `{ app: "tarja", version: 1, exportedAt, expenses, categories, income, budgets, groups }`. `exportedAt` is an ISO 8601 time. `expenses` are the stored fields as they are, one entry per expense and one per part of a plan, ordered by the day made, then `createdAt`, the part and the id. `categories` are the custom ones only (`id`, `name`, `color`, `icon`), since the built-in ones come with the app, `income` is the months the person set and `budgets` the rows of the limits as they are stored, ordered by month and then scope. Nothing in it is derived, so a later import can restore a database from it.
- **CSV** is only the expenses, for a spreadsheet: one row per expense or part of a plan, in the same order, with the columns date, text, category, amount, currency, payment, charge date, your part, part and group (empty for these, [below](#groups)). The header, the category name (the built-in names in the language of the app, "Other" for one that no longer exists) and the payment are in the app language. Days are `YYYY-MM-DD`. The amount is what was paid, in the decimals of the language without a currency symbol or grouping: Spanish uses `;` between columns and `12,50`, English `,` and `12.50`, as the delimiter follows the decimal mark. Your part is filled for a restaurant card expense only (the whole amount when none was set) and empty for the rest; the part is `2/3` for the second of three payments and empty for an expense that is not a plan. The file is UTF-8 with a byte order mark, so a spreadsheet reads the accents, and fields are quoted when they hold the delimiter, a quote or a line break.
- **A spreadsheet never runs a text.** A text or a category name that starts with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, a tab or a carriage return gets a leading `'`, so the cell is read as text and never as a formula.
- The file is `tarja-YYYY-MM-DD.json` or `.csv`, with the day of the device.

## Groups

- **JSON.** `groups` has one entry for each group and friend the device holds, closed and archived ones and those whose switch Show in my expenses is off included, in the order the server lists them (when they were made): `{ id, kind, name, currency, simplifyDebts, closed, archived, showInExpenses, pinned, me, members, entries }`, all as the server says: `name` is null for a friend, `closed` is `null`, `deleted` or `left`, `pinned` is whether the person pinned it to Expenses, and `me` is the member id of the person. A member is `{ id, name, role, claimed, leftAt, mergedInto }`, those who left and the placeholders merged into somebody included, in the group's order; an empty name is a person who deleted their account. An entry is as stored: `id`, `kind`, `text`, `categoryId`, `amountCents`, `currency`, `date`, `paidBy`, `split`, `shares` (a `memberId` and the `cents` of each, never the percentages), `payment`, `chargeDate`, `createdAt`, `updatedAt`, `deletedAt`, and `createdBy` and `updatedBy`, which are members (null once that person deleted their account); settlements are included and deleted entries left out, ordered by the day made, then `createdAt` and the id. Each field is written by name, so no email and no user id can get into the file. A group whose expenses have not reached the device is left out, as an empty list would say it has none. `expenses` stays the person's own expenses: their part of a shared expense is derived, never stored, and is never in it. The version stays 1, as nothing reads the file yet.
- **CSV.** The person's parts of the shared expenses are rows too, the ones the list shows ([shared expenses](shared-expenses.md)): only the groups whose switch is on, closed ones included, in the same order and with the same escaping and numbers. The last column, **group**, holds the name of the group, or the other person's for a friend, as the screens say it, and is empty for an expense of one's own. In a shared row **amount** is the total that was paid, **payment** is how the payer paid when the person is the payer ("Now" otherwise) with its **charge date**, **your part** is the person's part (an expense where it is zero gives no row), and **part** is empty. A settlement is not a row.
- **On the device.** Both files are built from what the device holds, so they work offline. Settings reads the groups and the expenses of each like any screen that adds them up, and while the groups, or the expenses of one of them, are still on their way for the first time, the two buttons are off and a `LiveNotice` (a `Notice` in a `role="status"` region that stays in the page) says what for. The wait is ten seconds at most (no request has a timeout of its own, and a connection that says it is up may stall): after that the buttons are on, and a group whose expenses have not arrived counts as one that cannot be had. When the expenses of a group cannot be had (the device never pulled them and the server is out of reach, there is no connection, or the wait ran out) that group is left out of both files, since an empty list would say it has none, the buttons are on and a `Notice` says so; if they arrive later the group goes in and the `Notice` goes away. A device that never heard of its groups has none to miss and says nothing, as it says nothing of its expenses.

## One group

The settings of a group lead to its table, a page of its own, to print, save as a PDF or download as CSV ([ADR-0043](../adr/0043-a-group-is-exported-as-one-table-the-same-for-everyone.md)). It is the same whoever makes it: nothing in it is the person's own. The logic is pure: the table in `core/group-table.ts`, the file in `core/export.ts`.

- **The table.** A row per entry that isn't deleted, settlements included, by the day made, then `createdAt` and the id, and a column per person: each cell is what the entry does to that person's balance, the whole amount for whoever paid less each share, a settlement too, so the one who paid it gains and the one paid loses. A cell is empty for a person who neither paid nor has a share, and zero for one who is in it and comes out even. A merged placeholder is read as the member it was merged into. People are named as the group names them (equal names numbered, "Deleted user" for an empty one), never "You" nor "(left)", and only the people in a row of the range, or holding an opening balance that isn't zero, have a column, in the group's order. A settlement's text is "Ana paid Bea", with no category; an expense with no text takes the name of its category, as in the ledger.
- **The filters.** The button and the sheet of the ledger's search and filters ([ADR-0042](../adr/0042-the-ledger-of-a-group-is-searched-and-filtered-from-one-button.md), [shared expenses](shared-expenses.md)): the search, Type, the category, From and To, Paid by and With, all empty at the start, which is the whole group, with the same status line under the title ("Showing x of y", with Clear) while something narrows the table. The search finds an entry by its text as the table writes it, its category and who paid, and by its whole amount and what it does to any person, not only to the one who searches. Entries after the To are left out. They are the page's state, lost on leaving it.
- **The last row.** Narrowed by the days alone, a From adds a first row, "Balance before <day>", with what each person held before that day, from every entry before it, and the last row, "Balance", is that plus the rows. Narrowed by anything else (the search, Type, the category, Paid by or With), there is no opening row and the last row is "Total of these expenses", each column the sum of the rows shown, since a balance would not be the sum of what is above it. Only the people in a row shown have a column then.
- **The page** (`/groups/$groupId/export`, depth 3, whose header's way back leads to the group): the group's name as its title, a hint, then **Print** and **Download CSV** with the button of the filters at the end of their row, the status line, then the table in a box that scrolls sideways when it is wider than the column (reachable with the keyboard, named by its caption: the days in words ("All the expenses", "From Nov 1, 2026", "Up to …", "Nov 1, 2026 to Nov 30, 2026"), then each filter in use after a "·": the type, the category, "Paid by Ana", "With Bea" and the search in quotes, the people named as in the table, never "You"; it is printed too, so paper says what it holds). The columns are the date (short), the expense (the row's header), the category, who paid, the amount and then the people; each person's cell is an `Amount` that always shows its sign, read aloud, and the color when Settings → Amounts allows it, with the symbol chosen there; the amount of the expense is neutral. A person's name may wrap in the header; the amounts don't. With no row and no opening balance, a `Notice` says "No expenses to show." and the actions are off. While the group's expenses haven't reached the device, the actions are off, and when they can't be had a `LiveNotice` says so, as on the screen of the group.
- **On paper** (`@media print`): the page alone, without the app's header, bottom bar, toasts or banner, nor the hint, the range and the actions; the title, the caption and the table, as wide as the sheet; a row is never cut between two sheets, the header of the table is repeated on each sheet while the closing balance, a row of the body and not a foot, is printed once at the end, and the colors are the light theme's whatever the theme. Print opens the browser's own dialog (`window.print()`), where saving a PDF is offered too.
- **CSV.** The same table: the columns date, expense, category, paid by, amount and currency, then one per person, named as above; a row per entry, then the balances, the opening one first when there is a From, each with its label in the expense column, the currency, and the cells. Each cell is signed (`-15.00`), empty for a person out of the entry. The numbers, the delimiter, the byte order mark, the quoting and the guard against formulas are those of the other CSV (a number is never guarded). The file is `tarja-<group>-YYYY-MM-DD.csv`: the name as the screens say it, without the characters a file name can't hold nor the invisible ones that change how it reads (control and format characters), its spaces as `-`, at most 60 characters (never half of one) and no `-` at the end, and the day of the device; `tarja-YYYY-MM-DD.csv` when nothing of the name is left.
- **On the device.** Built from what the device holds, the person's writes waiting to sync included, so it works offline and sends nothing.
