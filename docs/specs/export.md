# Exporting your data

Settings offers a JSON file with all your data and a CSV file with the expenses (export only, no import for now: [ADR-0001](../adr/0001-scope-of-the-app.md)). Both are built on the device from what it already holds, the persisted cache with the outbox on top (`applyPending`), so they work offline and send nothing to the server. Settings reads the expenses, the categories, the income and the budgets like any other screen does, so a device that never opened the summary still has its income and its budgets in the JSON; a device that has never heard from the server exports only what is waiting in its outbox. Deleted expenses are left out, and the logic is pure, in `core/export.ts`.

- **JSON** is all your data: `{ app: "tarja", version: 1, exportedAt, expenses, categories, income, budgets, groups }`. `exportedAt` is an ISO 8601 time. `expenses` are the stored fields as they are, one entry per expense and one per part of a plan, ordered by the day made, then `createdAt`, the part and the id. `categories` are the custom ones only (`id`, `name`, `color`, `icon`), since the built-in ones come with the app, `income` is the months the person set and `budgets` the rows of the limits as they are stored, ordered by month and then scope. Nothing in it is derived, so a later import can restore a database from it.
- **CSV** is only the expenses, for a spreadsheet: one row per expense or part of a plan, in the same order, with the columns date, text, category, amount, currency, payment, charge date, your part, part and group (empty for these, [below](#groups)). The header, the category name (the built-in names in the language of the app, "Other" for one that no longer exists) and the payment are in the app language. Days are `YYYY-MM-DD`. The amount is what was paid, in the decimals of the language without a currency symbol or grouping: Spanish uses `;` between columns and `12,50`, English `,` and `12.50`, as the delimiter follows the decimal mark. Your part is filled for a restaurant card expense only (the whole amount when none was set) and empty for the rest; the part is `2/3` for the second of three payments and empty for an expense that is not a plan. The file is UTF-8 with a byte order mark, so a spreadsheet reads the accents, and fields are quoted when they hold the delimiter, a quote or a line break.
- **A spreadsheet never runs a text.** A text or a category name that starts with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, a tab or a carriage return gets a leading `'`, so the cell is read as text and never as a formula.
- The file is `tarja-YYYY-MM-DD.json` or `.csv`, with the day of the device.

## Groups

- **JSON.** `groups` has one entry for each group and friend the device holds, closed and archived ones and those whose switch Show in my expenses is off included, in the order the server lists them (when they were made): `{ id, kind, name, currency, simplifyDebts, closed, archived, showInExpenses, me, members, entries }`, all as the server says: `name` is null for a friend, `closed` is `null`, `deleted` or `left`, and `me` is the member id of the person. A member is `{ id, name, role, claimed, leftAt, mergedInto }`, those who left and the placeholders merged into somebody included, in the group's order; an empty name is a person who deleted their account. An entry is as stored: `id`, `kind`, `text`, `categoryId`, `amountCents`, `currency`, `date`, `paidBy`, `split`, `shares` (a `memberId` and the `cents` of each, never the percentages), `payment`, `chargeDate`, `createdAt`, `updatedAt`, `deletedAt`, and `createdBy` and `updatedBy`, which are members (null once that person deleted their account); settlements are included and deleted entries left out, ordered by the day made, then `createdAt` and the id. Each field is written by name, so no email and no user id can get into the file. A group whose expenses have not reached the device is left out, as an empty list would say it has none. `expenses` stays the person's own expenses: their part of a shared expense is derived, never stored, and is never in it. The version stays 1, as nothing reads the file yet.
- **CSV.** The person's parts of the shared expenses are rows too, the ones the list shows ([shared expenses](shared-expenses.md)): only the groups whose switch is on, closed ones included, in the same order and with the same escaping and numbers. The last column, **group**, holds the name of the group, or the other person's for a friend, as the screens say it, and is empty for an expense of one's own. In a shared row **amount** is the total that was paid, **payment** is how the payer paid when the person is the payer ("Now" otherwise) with its **charge date**, **your part** is the person's part (an expense where it is zero gives no row), and **part** is empty. A settlement is not a row.
- **On the device.** Both files are built from what the device holds, so they work offline. Settings reads the groups and the expenses of each like any screen that adds them up, and while the groups, or the expenses of one of them, are still on their way for the first time, the two buttons are off and a `Notice` with `role="status"` says what for. The wait is ten seconds at most (no request has a timeout of its own, and a connection that says it is up may stall): after that the buttons are on, and a group whose expenses have not arrived counts as one that cannot be had. When the expenses of a group cannot be had (the device never pulled them and the server is out of reach, there is no connection, or the wait ran out) that group is left out of both files, since an empty list would say it has none, the buttons are on and a `Notice` says so; if they arrive later the group goes in and the `Notice` goes away. A device that never heard of its groups has none to miss and says nothing, as it says nothing of its expenses.
