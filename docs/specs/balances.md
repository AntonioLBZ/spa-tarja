# Balances and settling up

Each group keeps its own account. The decision is in [ADR-0012](../adr/0012-shared-expenses.md); the entries it is made from are in [shared expenses](shared-expenses.md).

## Rules

- **Balance** (`core/balance.ts`, pure). For every entry that isn't deleted, the member who paid is credited the whole amount and every member with a share is debited that share; a settlement is worked out the same way. A member's balance is credits minus debits, in cents: positive is owed to them, negative is what they owe. A group's balances add up to zero. They list every member in the group's order, those who left too, but not a merged placeholder (an id read as another member); an id the entries name that the device doesn't hold as a member yet (the groups not refreshed) comes last. Everything is computed on the device from the entries it holds, the unsynced ones on top, so it works offline; the server runs the same code to refuse leaving or deleting a group with a balance ([groups](groups.md)).
- **Who owes whom** (the default). For every entry, each member with a share, other than the one who paid, owes the payer that share. For each pair, what A owes B less what B owes A, the settlements included, is the debt between them: A owes B when it is positive, B owes A when negative, and a pair at zero isn't listed. A member's lines add up to their balance.
- **Simplified debts** (`core/simplify.ts`, pure), when the owner switches it on for the group: the same balances turned into payments by taking the member who owes most and the one who is owed most, paying the smaller of the two amounts, and repeating until all are zero; a tie goes to the first in the group's order, so every device gets the same payments. It makes at most one payment fewer than the members with a balance. Entries are not changed, and switching it off shows the pairs again.
- **A settlement** is an entry of kind `settlement` ([shared expenses](shared-expenses.md)): `paidBy` is the member who paid and the only share is the one who received. Its amount is at least one cent (zero and negative are refused) and may be more than the debt, which leaves the debt the other way round: if Ana owes Bruno €30 and pays him €50, Bruno owes Ana €20. Any member can record it between any two members who haven't left, so a group can always be settled.
- **Suggestions.** The lines of the view in use (who owes whom, or the simplified payments) are the settlements the screen suggests. Taking one fills in who, whom and how much; all three can be changed. Settling a simplified line is an ordinary settlement between the two people of that line: nothing else is touched, their balances move by it, and the payments are worked out again from the new balances, so paying less than a line can change the other lines too.
- **No color for debts.** Owing isn't an error, so the danger color isn't used; the words say it: "You owe", "owes you", "Settled" ([`DESIGN.md`](../../design-system/DESIGN.md#2-color-and-themes)).
- **Across groups.** The Groups screen adds the balances of the caller in each group: what they are owed in all, and what they owe in all, each from the groups where it is positive or negative. A friend counts as a group.
- **Merged, closed and deleted.** The id of a placeholder merged into a member is read as that member before anything is added ([groups](groups.md)), so a merge adds its balance to the target's, and a share that a merge leaves on the member who paid owes nobody. A closed group keeps the balances it had and offers no settle up. A member who deleted their account is a member with no name, shown as "Deleted user", whose balance the rest can still settle with a settlement ([authentication](authentication.md)).

## Screen

On the screen of a group ([groups](groups.md)), under the title:

- **The balance in words**: "You are owed €35.00", "You owe €12.50" or "All settled", as a `Notice`.
- **Balances**, a `Sheet` with a line for each debt of the view in use, the ones that involve you first, in words: "You owe Ana €20.00", "Bruno owes you €8.00", "Bruno owes Ana €5.00". Each line has Settle up as a ghost button. When the group is simplified, a quiet `Notice` above, with no role, says the debts are simplified and that a payment may name someone you didn't share with; it is there whenever the group is simplified, also with no debts and once it is closed. The debts are worked out once for the screen, and the lines and the form below read the same ones. The balance in words and the balance of each member are the same in both views. Under the lines, a closed `Disclosure`, "Everyone's balance", lists each member with their balance in words; a member who has left shows "(left)".
- **Settle up**, a button as wide as the column that opens a `Disclosure` form with: From and To (a `Select` of the members who haven't left, starting on you and on the one you owe most), the amount (a `Field`, with the debt of the view in use between the two as a hint when there is one), the day (today) and Save. There is no note: the entry's text is empty. From and To can't be the same, and the form says so under To. A hint under the amount says what follows: "After this, Bruno owes you €20.00" or "After this, you're settled with Bruno" (you may still owe others). It is what the lines of the view in use will show between the two once the settlement is saved, worked out over the entries with it on top, so in the simplified view it can differ from the debt minus the amount. The main button is off while the amount is zero or not a number, or From is To. Settle up on a line opens the form with that debt filled in and the focus on the amount; a line that names someone who left has none. The form is kept above the balances, so what was typed survives closing it, and saving empties it, closes it and gives the focus back to its button; a From or To who leaves, or is merged, while it is open goes back to where the form starts. It saves through the outbox, so it works offline, and the balances change at once; the settlement appears in the ledger as "You paid Bruno". Edit on a settlement of the ledger fills the form with it: its title reads "Edit the settlement" and takes the focus, the debts of the hint leave that settlement out, and Cancel sits beside Save, the two of one width. Save writes the same id and `createdAt` with an `updatedAt` later than the version it replaces; Save and Cancel put the form back to its start, closed, with the focus on the row of the settlement.
- The edit screen of the group has the switch for simplified debts: a `Checkbox`, "Simplify debts", with the hint "Shows the fewest payments that settle the group. It doesn't change any expense." Only the owner can change it, and it needs a connection ([offline sync](offline-sync.md)).
