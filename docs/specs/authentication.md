# Authentication

Google OAuth, no own passwords, through Better Auth mounted on Hono at `/api/auth/*`. Better Auth takes the D1 binding directly. It needs the `nodejs_als` compatibility flag (it uses `AsyncLocalStorage`).

- **Tables**: `user`, `session`, `account` and `verification`, in migration `0003`. They are written by hand, like the rest of the schema, and a test fails if Better Auth asks for a table or column the migration lacks.
- **Sessions**: an `httpOnly` cookie signed with `BETTER_AUTH_SECRET`, valid for 30 days. A 5 minute cookie cache lets most requests skip reading the session from D1. A revoked session can therefore stay valid for up to 5 minutes, but a deleted account can't: every route that needs a session also reads the `user` row by its key (`withUser`, `server/user.ts`) and answers `401` when it is gone. The cookie has no `Domain` (cross-subdomain cookies stay off), so it belongs to `tarja.albz.app` alone and the other sites under `albz.app` never see it.
- **Base URL**: the origin of each request, so the same code runs on `localhost`, `tarja.albz.app` and any other origin without configuration. Cloudflare only routes the Worker's own hosts, Google only redirects to the URIs registered in its console, and Better Auth rejects cross-origin `POST`s against that origin.
- **Access**: `/api/expenses`, `/api/categories`, `/api/income`, `/api/budgets`, `/api/profile`, `/api/groups` and `/api/invitations` answer `401` without a session; `/api/health` and `/api/auth/*` are public. The owner of every row is the Better Auth `user.id`. Any Google account can sign in. Rows saved while the login didn't exist belong to the old fixed user and are unreachable now.
- **Privacy.** The sign-in screen has a ghost link to the privacy notice, a `Dialog` ([settings](settings.md)), so it can be read before signing in.
- **Back to the invitation.** `startSignIn` sends the current path as the `callbackURL` when it is exactly the link of an invitation, `/join/` and a token of the token's shape with nothing after it (`invitationPath`), and `/` otherwise, so signing in from an invitation link lands on the link ([invitations](invitations.md)). Only a path of the app of that shape is ever sent, so the way back can't be pointed anywhere else. That path carries the signature of the link, and Better Auth keeps the `callbackURL` of a sign-in in its `verification` table for at most 10 minutes (longer if the sign-in is abandoned, until its record is cleaned up). That is the one place outside the invitation tables where a signed link is held. On such a link the sign-in screen says that signing in shows the invitation. The error callback stays `/?signin=failed`.
- **Client**: no Better Auth client, to keep the bundle small. Three calls (`src/lib/auth/service.ts`): `GET /api/auth/get-session`, `POST /api/auth/sign-in/social` (the answer carries the Google URL to go to; Google sends the person back to `/`, or to `/?signin=failed`) and `POST /api/auth/sign-out`. A small store (`createAuth`) holds the state: `loading`, `signed-out` (with `unreachable` when the server couldn't be asked) or `signed-in`. `AuthGate` shows the sign-in screen to everybody but a signed-in user, and `AppProviders` (`src/providers.tsx`) mounts the whole app inside it.
- **Offline**: the signed-in user's profile is kept in `localStorage`, so the app opens signed in with no network. Only a definite answer signs the user out: a `get-session` with no session, or a `401` from the API. A network error or a `5xx` never does. Without a cached profile and without a connection there is nothing to show but the sign-in screen, which says so.
- **Local data belongs to a user**: the two outboxes and the persisted cache are stored under the user id (`tarja:outbox:<id>`, `tarja:group-outbox:<id>`, `tarja:query-cache:<id>`; `createUserStorage`), so another account on the same browser never sees or pushes them. `UserDataProvider` builds them for the signed-in user and is remounted when the user changes. A `401` from any query or mutation makes the app ask `get-session` again (`createQueryClient({ onUnauthorized })`); an unsynced expense is kept, as a `401` is not a permanent refusal. Signing out deletes the user's local data only when nothing is waiting to sync in either outbox; otherwise it stays for the next sign-in of that user. Signing out needs a connection, because the cookie is `httpOnly` and only the server can end it. Data stored before the login existed sits under the old unscoped keys and is never read.
- **Secrets**: `BETTER_AUTH_SECRET`, `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID` and `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET`, in `.dev.vars` locally (`.dev.vars.example` lists them) and in `wrangler secret` in production. Google's console must list `<origin>/api/auth/callback/google` for each origin in use; in production that is `https://tarja.albz.app/api/auth/callback/google`.
- **Deleting the account** ([ADR-0014](../adr/0014-people-in-groups.md)). `DELETE /api/account` with `{ email }` (the account's own, typed to confirm, in any case and with spaces around it, by the check the dialog uses, `confirmsEmail` in `core/account.ts`; any other is `422 wrong-email` and deletes nothing) needs a connection and a session, and does everything in one D1 batch, all or nothing. It answers `204` with no body, and expires the cookies of the session through Better Auth's own sign-out. No balance holds it back: the balances stay in the ledgers, below.
    - It deletes the user's `expenses`, `categories`, `income`, `budget` and `profile`, and revokes the open invitations they made. An invitation they accepted stays accepted, by nobody.
    - In the groups they were in, open or closed, their member keeps its row but loses the user and the name (`user_id` null, `name` empty), so the screens show "Deleted user", and the entries are left as they are: they are also the record of the others, and the balances don't move. `created_by`, `updated_by` and the other user columns of the shared tables are set to null. The rest of the group can settle that member's balance with a settlement, or remove them once it is zero, like a placeholder.
    - A group they owned goes to the member who is a user, hasn't left and joined first (by `claimed_at`), open or closed. When there is none, the group is deleted and closed, and it has no owner from then on, since nobody is left to act for it. A friend whose other side is a placeholder or a deleted user is deleted the same way; one whose other side uses the app stays open, owned by that person. In the batch, the open invitations are revoked first, then those groups close, the user steps down as owner before anybody takes over (a group has one owner), their members lose the name, their own data goes, and the `user` row goes last, since their members are found by their `user_id` until then.
    - Then it deletes the `user` row, and the sessions and accounts (with the Google tokens) go with it through their cascades, so every session of the user, on every device, answers `401` at once, also one held by its cookie cache (above). Nothing in `verification` belongs to a user: it holds the state of sign-ins on their way, which expires by itself. The browser forgets the cookie, the device deletes its local data under the user id, and the sign-in screen shows.
    - It is irreversible. The screen offers the export first ([settings](settings.md)), and says that the backups D1 keeps for recovery expire on their own. The privacy notice, which says all this, is in the settings and on the sign-in screen ([settings](settings.md)).
