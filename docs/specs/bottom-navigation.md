# Bottom navigation

On a phone the destinations are a bar at the bottom; from 40 rem up they stay in the header ([header](header.md)). Four words and the logo don't fit the width of a phone, and the thumb reaches the bottom of a tall phone ([`DESIGN.md`](../../design-system/DESIGN.md#1-principles)). The bar also holds, in the middle, the "+" that adds an expense ([ADR-0016](../adr/0016-forms-for-a-task-open-in-a-sheet.md), its look in [ADR-0017](../adr/0017-the-look-of-the-plus.md)).

## Rules

- **Four destinations and an action**, in this order: Expenses (`/`), Groups (`/groups`), the "+", Summary (`/summary`) and Settings (`/settings`). The "+" is not a destination but a button that opens the add form in a sheet ([expenses](expenses.md)), so it is never the current one. The charts, the budgets, the categories, a group and its settings are deeper screens with no tab of their own: the tab of the screen they are reached from is the current one (the charts and the budgets under Summary, the categories under Settings, a group and its settings under Groups). `navSection` in `lib/route-transition` says which.
- **One navigation at a time is in the page.** A hook on the media query `(width >= 40rem)` decides whether the page has the header words or the bottom bar, so assistive technology and the tests never meet two sets of the same links. Tests that don't say otherwise get the wide one.
- **Below 40 rem** the bar is drawn and the header holds the logo, centered, which leads home, and, on a screen deeper than a tab, a link back to its parent at the left ([header](header.md)). **From 40 rem** the bar isn't drawn and the header holds Expenses on the left, the logo and Groups, Summary and Settings on the right, with the same underline as now. The "+" is then the text action "Add expense" in the header, next to Expenses ([header](header.md)): there is no floating button, and the page keeps no room at the bottom for one.
- **Expenses** leads home like the header word. Every tab leads to its screen, and pressing the current one scrolls the page to the top. None of them touches the add form.
- **Going between tabs** fades like any two screens at the same depth; going deeper slides ([`DESIGN.md`](../../design-system/DESIGN.md#6-motion)). Groups is depth 1, a group 2, its settings 3, `/join/$token` 1.
- **Nothing is hidden behind it.** The page has room at the bottom for the bar, and whatever is anchored to the bottom, the toast first, sits above the bar and the safe area. The bar is hidden while a text field or a `Select` has the focus (not a checkbox or a radio, and not a field in a sheet or a dialog, which covers the bar and has to leave the "+" able to take the focus back when it closes), so the keyboard doesn't leave it floating: it slides out and is no longer in the tab order, and it comes back when the focus leaves. The "+" is part of the bar, so it goes with it. Known limit: it is the focus that decides, not the keyboard, so where a phone closes the keyboard and leaves the focus in the field (the back button of Android), the bar stays out until the focus leaves; it is checked on a real phone before the merge.

## Screen

- `BottomNav`, a shared component (`src/components/bottom-nav`, with its story), fixed to the bottom on the paper (`--color-sheet`) with a hairline on top, `--bottom-nav-height` (4.5rem) tall, plus `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`, and padded at the sides by the safe areas of a landscape phone. It is a `<nav>` with a list of five columns of equal width: the four links and the "+" in the middle one. Each link is an icon over its word, at `--font-size-xs`; the icon is decorative (`aria-hidden`) and the word is always there. A link is `--bottom-nav-height` tall, so above `--size-touch`.
- The current tab has `aria-current="page"`, its word in weight 900 and, for a mark that doesn't depend on color, a pill (`--color-surface-strong`) behind its icon. One pill, not one per tab: it **glides** to the new tab, first stretching over both and then settling (GSAP, only under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`; without it, and before the script runs, the current tab's icon draws its own pill).
- The **"+"** is a `<button>` in the middle of the list, with `aria-haspopup="dialog"` and no `aria-current`. It is a 3.5 rem squircle (`--radius`: 1.25 rem) in `--color-spark` with the `add` icon in `--color-on-spark`, raised `--bottom-nav-lift` over the bar and cut from it by a ring of the paper color, over the word "Add" ("Añadir") in weight 900. The whole column is the target, not only the squircle. Its accessible name is "Add expense" ("Añadir gasto"), which holds the word that shows. While pressed it scales and its icon turns a quarter.
- Icons: `receipt_long` (Expenses), `groups` (Groups), `bar_chart` (Summary) and `settings` (Settings), and `add` for the "+", in the interface set (`UI_ICONS`), outside the category icons. `groups` and `person` are also the icons of a group and a friend in the lists.
- The component takes its items by props: label, path and icon, and, for the "+", an `action` (label, icon and what to do when it is pressed); it knows the router the way `ButtonLink` does and reads no messages ([frontend](frontend.md)).
- The words in Spanish are "Gastos", "Grupos", "Resumen" and "Ajustes". At 320 px each column is 64 px wide, and nothing overflows in either language, with the fonts loaded or not. Check it again when a word changes.
