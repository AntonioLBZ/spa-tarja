# Bottom navigation

On a phone the destinations are a bar at the bottom; from 40 rem up they stay in the header ([header](header.md)). Four words and the logo don't fit the width of a phone, and the thumb reaches the bottom of a tall phone ([`DESIGN.md`](../../design-system/DESIGN.md#1-principles)). The bar also holds, in the middle, the "+" that adds an expense ([ADR-0016](../adr/0016-forms-for-a-task-open-in-a-sheet.md)).

## Rules

- **Four destinations and an action**, in this order: Expenses (`/`), Groups (`/groups`), the "+", Summary (`/summary`) and Settings (`/settings`). The "+" is not a destination but a button that opens the add form in a sheet ([expenses](expenses.md)), so it is never the current one. The charts, the budgets, the categories, a group and its settings are deeper screens with no tab of their own: the tab of the screen they are reached from is the current one (the charts and the budgets under Summary, the categories under Settings, a group and its settings under Groups). `navSection` in `lib/route-transition` says which.
- **One navigation at a time is in the page.** A hook on the media query `(width >= 40rem)` decides whether the page has the header words or the bottom bar, so assistive technology and the tests never meet two sets of the same links. Tests that don't say otherwise get the wide one.
- **Below 40 rem** the bar is drawn and the header holds the logo, centered, which leads home, and, on a screen deeper than a tab, a link back to its parent at the left ([header](header.md)). **From 40 rem** the bar isn't drawn and the header holds Expenses on the left, the logo and Groups, Summary and Settings on the right, with the same underline as now. The "+" is then a floating button at the lower-right corner of the content column, above the safe area, with the page keeping room for it at the bottom; one of the two, the bar's or the floating one, is in the page at a time.
- **Expenses** leads home like the header word. Every tab leads to its screen, and pressing the current one scrolls the page to the top. None of them touches the add form.
- **Going between tabs** fades like any two screens at the same depth; going deeper slides ([`DESIGN.md`](../../design-system/DESIGN.md#6-motion)). Groups is depth 1, a group 2, its settings 3, `/join/$token` 1.
- **Nothing is hidden behind it.** The page has room at the bottom for the bar, and whatever is anchored to the bottom, the toast first, sits above the bar and the safe area. The bar is hidden while a text field or a `Select` has the focus (not a checkbox or a radio), so the keyboard doesn't leave it floating: it slides out and is no longer in the tab order, and it comes back when the focus leaves. The "+" is part of the bar, so it goes with it. Known limit: it is the focus that decides, not the keyboard, so where a phone closes the keyboard and leaves the focus in the field (the back button of Android), the bar stays out until the focus leaves; it is checked on a real phone before the merge.

## Screen

- `BottomNav`, a shared component (`src/components/bottom-nav`, with its story), fixed to the bottom on `--color-bg-secondary` with an ink hairline on top, `--bottom-nav-height` (3.75rem) tall, plus `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`, and padded at the sides by the safe areas of a landscape phone. It is a `<nav>` with a list: the links, of equal width, and the "+" in a column of its own, 3rem wide, in the middle. Each is an icon over its word, at `--font-size-s`; the icon is decorative (`aria-hidden`) and the word is always there. A link is `--bottom-nav-height` tall, so above `--size-touch`.
- The current tab has `aria-current="page"` and, for a mark that doesn't depend on color, a bar of `--size-1` in ink along the top edge of its box, the counterpart of the underline of the header. No fill and no accent: the accent marks what can be acted on. It does not slide between tabs: the bar holds still under its own view transition name while the page fades, and the mark changes to the new tab.
- The **"+"** is a `<button>` in the middle of the list, named "Add an expense" ("Añadir un gasto") with no word under it and `aria-haspopup="dialog"`: the primary `Button` as a `glyph` (the accent fill, `--size-touch` square) with the `add` icon, centered in the bar. It has no `aria-current`.
- Icons: `receipt_long` (Expenses), `groups` (Groups), `bar_chart` (Summary) and `settings` (Settings), and `add` for the "+", in the interface set (`UI_ICONS`), outside the category icons. `groups` and `person` are also the icons of a group and a friend in the lists.
- The component takes its items by props: label, path and icon, and, for the "+", an `action` (label, icon and what to do when it is pressed); it knows the router the way `ButtonLink` does and reads no messages ([frontend](frontend.md)).
- The words in Spanish are "Gastos", "Grupos", "Resumen" and "Ajustes". At 320 px each tab is 68 px wide (the four share what the 48 px column of the "+" leaves), and nothing overflows in either language, with the fonts loaded or not; the 8-letter English words, "Expenses" and "Settings", are the tight ones, 64 px at `--font-size-s` in the monospace face.
