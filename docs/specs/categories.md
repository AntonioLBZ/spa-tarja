# Categories

## Rules

- Default categories (initial proposal: food, groceries, leisure, bills, housing/rent, transport, health, other) are read-only. They live in `core/` with stable ids; their names come from the locale files, not from the database. Custom categories live in D1, so `expenses.category_id` has no foreign key to the defaults.
- **Custom categories** live in the `categories` table (`id` a UUID made on the client, `user_id`, `name`, `name_key`, `created_at`). Unlike expenses, managing them needs a connection: `GET /categories`, an idempotent `PUT /categories/:id` that creates or renames, and `DELETE /categories/:id`, which really deletes the row. Offline-first buys little for something done rarely, and it would need a second outbox with its own ordering. The list sits in the persisted query cache, so an offline device can still file expenses under the categories it has seen.
- **Color and icon.** Every category has a color key and an icon key from fixed lists ([`DESIGN.md`](../../design-system/DESIGN.md#category-colors)). The defaults carry theirs in `core/`. Custom categories get `color` and `icon` columns in `categories`, in a migration of their own that gives the existing rows the default color and the generic icon; the manager chooses them next to the name and the server accepts only keys of those lists. An expense never stores them: it only has `category_id`, and a category that no longer exists shows as "Other", with its color and icon.
- **An expense is never refused for its category.** The server checks only the shape of `category_id` (a default id or a UUID), never that the row exists: the category may have been deleted on another device, or created while this one was offline. An expense whose category no longer exists shows as "Other" and keeps its id.
- Names are trimmed, up to 40 characters, and unique per user ignoring case and accents (`name_key`, unique with `user_id`); a clash answers `409 name-taken`. The app also checks them against the default names of the current language.
- Auto-selection is a dictionary of normalized keywords (lowercase, no accents) in English and Spanish that runs on the client, with the logic in `core/`. The user can change it on each expense.
- **From the person's own history.** Only when the keywords find nothing, so the suggestion would be "Other", the words of the typed text are looked up in the expenses already on the device (`core/history-suggestion.ts`). A word counts when it has at least 4 letters, whole and ignoring case and accents, and every earlier expense that has it is in the same category; a word seen in several categories is ignored, which also drops filler like "con" or "with". The category with the most such words wins and, on a tie, the one with the most earlier expenses that carry them, a plan counting once. Deleted expenses and categories that no longer exist are skipped, and a custom category can be suggested, which the keywords never do. It runs on the client from the local copy, with no storage, request or screen of its own, and the hint is the one of a keyword suggestion. A category picked by hand is never replaced. A category the person corrected is the one stored on the expense, so the history is how the app learns from corrections.

## Screen

- `/categories`, reached from a section of the settings that says what categories are for. At the top is the form that adds a category: its name beside a preview of its color and icon, the button that adds it and, under them, "Color and icon" behind a disclosure, closed until pressed. Under the form, the person's own categories on a `Sheet` labeled "Yours" and the built-in ones on another, labeled "Built-in", with a line above it that says they can't be changed. While the person has none of their own, the first sheet gives way to a quiet notice that says so.
- The sheets are those of the expenses ([`DESIGN.md`](../../design-system/DESIGN.md#shape)): the rows have no notch, and show the icon and the name and, for the person's own, the chevron at the end. The built-in rows have nothing to open, so they have no chevron.
- A row of the person's own is a disclosure: pressing it opens, right under it, Edit and Delete as ghost buttons `--size-4` apart, and pressing it again closes them. Rows open one by one, not as an accordion. The panel comes in with a fade and a short vertical shift over `--duration-base`, like a new expense.
- Edit loads the category into the form at the top, as Edit does for an expense: the row closes, the page scrolls to the form (at once under reduced motion) and the focus goes to the name field. While it edits, the label of the field reads "Edit category", the main button "Save" and Cancel sits under the form as a ghost; the picker is closed and starts from the color and icon of the category. Saving or cancelling puts the form back to adding, empty, and gives the focus back to the toggle of the row that was edited. Pressing Edit on another row loads that one instead.
- Delete asks in the panel, with the question, and Delete and Cancel as ghost buttons, and says what happens to the expenses. The focus goes to Cancel and, when it closes, back to Delete.
- Offline, the rows still open but the buttons in the panel are off, and so are the field and the buttons of the form, with the notice at the top saying why.
- **In a group.** An entry of a group keeps the category the person who made it picked, a default one or one of their own, and the suggestion is the same. A custom category exists only for its owner, so the other members of the group see "Other" with its color and icon, as for any category that no longer exists. Each person's own category for a shared expense is left for later ([shared expenses](shared-expenses.md)).
