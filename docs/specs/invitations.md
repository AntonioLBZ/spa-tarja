# People and invitations

The decision is in [ADR-0014](../adr/0014-people-in-groups.md). Everything here needs a connection ([offline sync](offline-sync.md)). Merging a dummy is in [groups](groups.md); deleting an account is in [authentication](authentication.md).

## Rules

- **Username** (migration `0010`). A user may set one, 3 to 30 characters of `a-z`, `0-9`, `.`, `_` and `-`, stored in lowercase; capitals typed are lowercased. It is unique among users. The table `profile` has one row per user who set one; removing it deletes the row, so "no row" is the default. The handle of a user is the username when there is one and the email of their account otherwise. Nobody sees another user's username or email in a group: groups show the name of the member.
- **Looking someone up** (`GET /people/lookup?handle=`) takes a handle, an exact match ignoring case, and answers `{ name, you }` where `name` is the name of the Google account, or `404`. It never matches a part of a handle, and it answers only to a signed-in user. The email is matched when it has an `@`, the username otherwise; an account with a username set is not found by its email. Nothing else about the account is answered.
- **Profile API**: `GET /profile` answers `{ username, handle }` for the caller; `PUT /profile` with `{ username }` (null removes it) answers the profile, `409 username-taken` or `400`.
- **An invitation** (table `shared_invitations`, migration `0012`) has a `token` (random, 128 bits, in the link), the `group_id`, a `member_id`, a `target_user_id`, `created_by`, `created_at`, `expires_at` (14 days after), and when it was accepted, declined or revoked. It comes in three kinds, by what is null:
    - **For nobody in particular** (`member_id` and `target_user_id` null): a link anyone can use once. Whoever accepts becomes a new member, named after their Google account unless they change it.
    - **For a placeholder** (`member_id` set, a placeholder nobody has claimed and that hasn't left or been merged): whoever accepts takes its place, keeping the name the group gave it unless they change it, and everything in the ledger that names it.
    - **For a user** (`target_user_id` set, with or without a `member_id`): the same, but only that user can accept, and it shows in their Groups screen.

    A placeholder has at most one open invitation: making another revokes the first. At most 30 are open in a group.

- **Making one**: `POST /groups/:groupId/invitations` with `{}` makes a link for nobody in particular, with `{ memberId }` one for that placeholder, and `{ handle }`, with or without `memberId`, one for that user, answering `404` when nobody has the handle and `409 already-member` when that user is already in the group. Any member of an open group can make one. It answers `{ token, expiresAt }`; the link is `<origin>/join/<token>`. `GET /groups/:groupId/invitations` lists the open ones of the group (the tokens too, so the link can be copied again) and `DELETE /invitations/:token` revokes one (its maker or the owner).
- **Receiving one**: `GET /invitations` lists the open ones for the caller, made for their handle, with the group's name, who invited and the name of the member if there is one. `GET /invitations/:token` shows an invitation to whoever has the link while signed in: the group's name, who invited, the name of the member (or none) and how many people are in; no entries. `POST /invitations/:token/accept` with an optional `{ name }` makes the caller a member as above; `POST /invitations/:token/decline` closes it. Answers: `410 gone` when it doesn't exist, expired, was revoked, used or declined, the placeholder left or was merged, the group is closed, or it was made for another user; `409 already-member` when the caller is already in that group (they should open the group); `409 too-many-groups` over the limit. Accepting is atomic with marking the invitation, so a link serves one person.
- **A dummy that turns out to be a person who joined on their own**: if somebody joined by a link for nobody in particular and the group already had a placeholder for them, the owner merges the placeholder into the new member ([groups](groups.md)). That is why a link for nobody in particular needs no placeholder first.
- **Sign-in from a link.** Opening `/join/<token>` signed out shows the sign-in screen, and the Google round trip comes back to that same address: `startSignIn` sends the current path as the `callbackURL` when it starts with `/join/` and `/` otherwise ([authentication](authentication.md)). The path is never taken from outside the app.
- **Not guessable, not forever**: a token that isn't found, is expired, was revoked or isn't for the caller answers the same `410`, so a token can't be told from a missing one. Looking up handles and previewing tokens are the two places a stranger could probe; a rate limit on both (Cloudflare's own rules, free) is to be considered at the end of the work, and is noted in `TODO.md`.

## Screen

- **Settings → Profile** (a section after the language and the theme): a `Field` for the username with the hint "People find you by this. Until you pick one, they find you by your email.", Save and, once there is one, Remove, with the handle in use shown under it ("People find you as ana@example.com"). A taken name or a bad one is an error under the field. Offline the field and its buttons are off with a notice.
- **Invite**, in the edit screen of a group: above the sheet of people, a ghost button "Invite someone" copies a link for nobody in particular (or opens the share sheet of the phone where there is one, `navigator.share`), and the open invitations are listed under it ("Open link, until 15 Nov" with Copy link and Revoke). In the panel of a placeholder, the ghost button Invite offers the same for that placeholder, "Copy invitation link" and "Invite by username or email". The second asks for a handle and shows who it is ("Invite Ana Pérez?") before sending it; the row says "Invited Ana Pérez" with Revoke. The form of Add a friend has the same two choices. Each can be repeated.
- **`/join/$token`** (depth 1): a sheet that says "<Name> invited you to <group>", the name you'll have in it (a `Field`, filled with the placeholder's name or with your Google name) and Join and Decline. Joining opens the group. An invitation that is gone says so in a `Notice` with a link to Groups. Waiting invitations for the caller also appear in Groups ([groups](groups.md)).
