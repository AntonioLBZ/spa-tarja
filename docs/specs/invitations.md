# People and invitations

The decision is in [ADR-0014](../adr/0014-usernames-and-invitations.md). Everything here needs a connection ([offline sync](offline-sync.md)).

## Rules

- **Username** (migration `0010`). A user may set one, 3 to 30 characters of `a-z`, `0-9`, `.`, `_` and `-`, stored in lowercase; capitals typed are lowercased. It is unique among users. The table `profile` has one row per user who set one; removing it deletes the row, so "no row" is the default. The handle of a user is the username when there is one and the email of their account otherwise. Nobody sees another user's username or email in a group: groups show the name of the member.
- **Looking someone up** (`GET /people/lookup?handle=`) takes a handle, an exact match ignoring case, and answers `{ name, you }` where `name` is the name of the Google account, or `404`. It never matches a part of a handle, and it answers only to a signed-in user. The email is matched when it has an `@`, the username otherwise; an account with a username set is not found by its email. Nothing else about the account is answered.
- **Profile API**: `GET /profile` answers `{ username, handle }` for the caller; `PUT /profile` with `{ username }` (null removes it) answers the profile, `409 username-taken` or `400`.
- **An invitation claims a member.** Table `shared_invitations`: `token` (random, 128 bits, in the link), `group_id`, `member_id` (a placeholder that nobody has claimed and that hasn't left), `target_user_id` (null for a link, else the user it is for), `created_by`, `created_at`, `expires_at` (14 days after), and when it was accepted, declined or revoked. A member has at most one open invitation: making another revokes the first. At most 30 are open in a group.
- **Making one**: `POST /groups/:groupId/invitations` with `{ memberId }` makes a link; with `{ memberId, handle }` makes one for that user and answers `404` when nobody has the handle, `409 already-member` when that user is already in the group. Any member of the group can make one. It answers `{ token, expiresAt }`; the link is `<origin>/join/<token>`. `GET /groups/:groupId/invitations` lists the open ones of the group (the tokens too, so the link can be copied again) and `DELETE /invitations/:token` revokes one (its maker or the owner).
- **Receiving one**: `GET /invitations` lists the open ones for the caller, made by handle, with the group's name, who invited and the name of the member. `GET /invitations/:token` shows an invitation to whoever has the link while signed in: the group's name, who invited, the name of the member and how many people are in; no entries. `POST /invitations/:token/accept` with an optional `{ name }` makes the caller the member (`user_id`, `claimed_at`), who keeps the name the group used unless the body renames it; `POST /invitations/:token/decline` closes it. Answers: `410 gone` when it doesn't exist, expired, was revoked, used or declined, the member left, or it was made for another user; `409 already-member` when the caller is already in that group (they should open the group); `409 too-many-groups` over the limit. Accepting is atomic with marking the invitation, so a link serves one person.
- **Sign-in from a link.** Opening `/join/<token>` signed out shows the sign-in screen, and the Google round trip comes back to that same address: `startSignIn` sends the current path as the `callbackURL` when it starts with `/join/` and `/` otherwise ([authentication](authentication.md)). The path is never taken from outside the app.
- **Not guessable, not forever**: a token that isn't found, is expired, was revoked or isn't for the caller answers the same `410`, so a token can't be told from a missing one. Looking up handles and previewing tokens are the two places a stranger could probe; a rate limit on both (Cloudflare's own rules, free) is to be considered at the end of the work, and is noted in `TODO.md`.

## Screen

- **Settings → Profile** (a section after the language and the theme): a `Field` for the username with the hint "People find you by this. Until you pick one, they find you by your email.", Save and, once there is one, Remove, with the handle in use shown under it ("People find you as ana@example.com"). A taken name or a bad one is an error under the field. Offline the field and its buttons are off with a notice.
- **Invite** (the panel of a placeholder in the edit screen of a group, and the form of Add a friend): two ghost buttons, "Copy invitation link" and "Invite by username or email". The first makes the invitation and copies the link (or opens the share sheet of the phone where there is one, `navigator.share`), and the row says "Invited, until 15 Nov" with Copy link and Revoke. The second asks for a handle and shows who it is ("Invite Ana Pérez?") before sending it; the row says "Invited Ana Pérez" with Revoke. Both can be repeated.
- **`/join/$token`** (depth 1): a sheet that says "<Name> invited you to <group>", the member it will make you ("You'll join as <name>", a `Field` to change it) and Join and Decline. Joining opens the group. An invitation that is gone says so in a `Notice` with a link to Groups. Waiting invitations for the caller also appear in Groups ([groups](groups.md)).
