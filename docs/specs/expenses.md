# Expenses

## Rules

- An edit is the same idempotent `PUT /expenses/:id` as a new expense, one per row, through the outbox, so it needs no endpoint or migration and works offline. The add form loads the expense (`draftOf`) and `reviseExpenses` (`src/features/expenses/create-expenses.ts`) turns what it holds into the writes. Each row keeps its `id`, `created_at` and `currency`, and its `updated_at` is the later of now and one more than the newest `updated_at` it replaces, so a device whose clock runs behind still wins over the version it edited instead of losing the edit silently ([offline sync](offline-sync.md)). Delete takes the same rule.
- A plan is edited whole, charged parts included. Its parts keep their ids by part number; a part it gains is a new row under the same `plan_id`, made when the plan was so the plan keeps its place in the list, and a part it loses is saved as deleted. A plan turned into one payment or into a card payment is its first part with no plan; an expense turned into a plan becomes the first part of a new plan. A plan can be edited only when every part of it is on the device.
- Your part goes only with a restaurant card: an edit that pays it another way sends `personalCents: null`, since the API answers 400 otherwise and the outbox drops a 400 for good.
- The category stays the saved id, even one whose category was deleted (it shows as "Other" and is kept unless the person picks another); the suggestion from the text never replaces it.
- Known limits, acceptable for one person: a plan edited on two devices before they sync can end up with parts of both edits, because each part wins on its own; and an edit made after a delete on another device brings the expense back, as the newer write.
- **Undo after a delete.** Delete keeps no confirmation, so it can be taken back for a few seconds, or until the person dismisses the toast (the `Toast` of [`DESIGN.md`](../../design-system/DESIGN.md#components)). `restoreExpenses` writes every deleted part again with `deleted_at` null and an `updated_at` of the later of now and one more than the deletion, the later-than rule of an edit, so it wins over the delete on every device. It goes through the outbox like any write: offline it replaces the entry of the delete that is still waiting, so the server never hears of either, and after the delete has synced it is one more `PUT`. A plan comes back whole. Only the last delete can be undone: there is no history.
- The decisions behind Edit, Duplicate and Delete are in [ADR-0010](../adr/0010-edit-duplicate-and-delete-expenses.md).
- **Duplicating an expense** is the add form loaded with an expense (`draftOf`) and saved as a new one, so it needs no endpoint or migration and works offline like any new expense. `createExpenses` gives every part a new id and a plan a new `plan_id`, and keeps the currency of the original. The day is today and `created_at` is now; a deferred payment takes the charge date the form proposes for that day; a plan keeps its number of payments and its amounts, on dates counted from today. It opens the form and saves nothing by itself ([ADR-0010](../adr/0010-edit-duplicate-and-delete-expenses.md)), replaces a half typed expense without asking, and, like Edit, is only for a plan with every part on the device (`canEdit`). `ExpenseSheetProvider` holds it as `copying`, next to `editing`, and never both at once.

## Screen

- The add form opens in a **sheet** ([ADR-0016](../adr/0016-forms-for-a-task-open-in-a-sheet.md)): a `Dialog` of the `sheet` variant, a bottom sheet below 40 rem and a centered dialog from it. The "+" opens it from every screen ([ADR-0017](../adr/0017-the-look-of-the-plus.md)): the action in the middle of the phone bottom bar ([bottom navigation](bottom-navigation.md)), the text action "Add expense" in the header from 40 rem ([header](header.md)). The title of the dialog is the title of the form, and a close button ends its title row. It opens with the focus on Amount. Closing it (the close button, Escape, the back button of a phone) keeps what was typed, so the next "+" finds the form as it was; a form whose amount and description are empty is untouched and starts again. Saving a new expense closes the sheet and the next "+" starts a new, empty form. Away from the home screen a `Toast` says "Expense added" (on it the new row shows in the list); a budget warning replaces that toast. The sheet is built on the first "+" and stays in the page afterwards, so a draft survives. Whether it is open lives in memory only. The header's Expenses and the logo only lead home: neither opens nor collapses the form; only its "Add expense" opens it.
- With "Restaurant card" as the payment, the form asks "Your part" under the charge date, an amount like the others; empty means all of it was yours. "Amount" keeps its label: it is what the card paid. While your part is less than the amount, a hint under the field says "Others pay back" and the difference; a part over the amount marks the field invalid, with a message under it, and the expense is not saved. Choosing another payment clears it, and installments stay offered only for "Now".
- The list on the home screen, a group at a time: by day unless the person picks another grouping (see [the expense list](expense-list.md)). An expense is a row with the badge of its category, its name, its amount and what else there is to say: the category when the name is the person's own text, how and when it is charged, what the card paid ("Paid" and the amount) when your part of a restaurant card is less than that, and "Not synced yet". The amount of a restaurant card expense is your part. A plan is one row on the purchase day, with its total.
- A row is a disclosure, like the rows of the categories: the whole row is the toggle, so the notch is the toggle's box, and a chevron at its end says it can be pressed. Pressing it opens a panel right under it and pressing it again closes the panel. Rows open one by one, not as an accordion: each keeps its own state, and an open row stays open while expenses come in around it.
- The panel holds what can be done to the expense: Edit, Duplicate, then Delete, as ghost buttons `--size-4` apart. Delete is one press, with no confirmation: the expense, or every part of its plan, is deleted at once, and the row fades out before it goes. A `Toast` says "Deleted <name>" (the text of the expense, else its category) and offers Undo for 8 s; the next delete replaces it, so only the last one can be taken back. Undo brings the expense, or the whole plan, back with the animation of a new expense and the focus goes to its row. The focus doesn't stay on a button that is leaving: it goes at once to the toggle of the next row, or of the one before when the row was the last, or to the list title when no row is left. The panel comes in with a fade and a short vertical shift over `--duration-base`, like the one of a category.
- Edit loads the expense, or the whole plan, into the add form and opens the sheet. The focus goes to the dialog itself, never to a field, so a phone doesn't open its keyboard. While it edits, the title reads "Edit the expense", the main button "Save", and Cancel sits `--size-4` beside it; nothing else in the form changes. The fields hold what was saved: the charge date, your part, the number of payments and the amount of each. The category stays the saved one, with no suggestion; one that no longer exists shows as "Other" and is kept unless another is picked. Save or Cancel puts the form back to adding, empty, and the focus back on the row, or on the list title when the row is gone. Closing the sheet (its close button, Escape) ends an edit as Cancel does, and deleting the expense being edited also puts the form back to adding. A half typed new expense is replaced by the edit without asking. For a plan whose payments aren't all on the device yet, Edit is off and a quiet notice under the buttons says why. Duplicate loads the expense, or the whole plan, into the same form as a new one: the text, the category, the amount, how it is paid and your part are the original's, the day is today, a deferred payment is charged on the day the form proposes for it, and a plan keeps its number of payments and the amount of each, on dates counted from today. The sheet opens with the focus on the dialog as in Edit; the title reads "Duplicate the expense", the main button "Add" and Cancel sits `--size-4` beside it. Nothing is saved until Add, so it never copies in one tap, and a half typed expense is replaced without asking. Add, Cancel or closing the sheet puts the form back to adding, empty, with the focus on the row it was copied from, or on the list title when that row is gone. Duplicate is off for the same plans as Edit, under the same notice.

## Sharing an expense

The form gains a choice, **For**: Just me, the form as described above, or Shared, which swaps your part and the installments for who it is shared with, who paid and how it is split, and keeps the payment and the charge date when you are the one who paid. Everything else (the description, the amount, the category and its suggestion, the day) is the same and is kept when switching. The rules and the fields are in [shared expenses](shared-expenses.md), the screens of a group in [groups](groups.md). Edit, Duplicate and Delete of a shared row work as above, with the three differences that they act on the entry of the group for every member, that an edit keeps the group, and that the toast names the group.
