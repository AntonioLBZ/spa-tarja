# Settings

- `/settings`, linked from the header. One column of sections, `--size-8` apart: the language, the theme (Light, Dark, then System) and the app color (each a `ChoiceGroup`, see [Color and themes](../../design-system/DESIGN.md#2-color-and-themes)), Profile, Categories (a hint and a link to `/categories`, as wide as the column), Your data, and the account (its Sign out button as wide as the column).
- **Your data** has a heading, a hint that says what the files hold and that they are made on the device, so they work offline, and two `Button`s, all your data (JSON) and only the expenses (CSV). While both fit on one line they are equal and side by side, `--size-4` apart; when they don't, they stack, each as wide as the column. A press downloads the file at once, with nothing to confirm and no state to show: the browser says that it saved it. Both are on, offline too, except while the groups load for the first time: then both are off and a notice says what they wait for, and when the expenses of a group can't be had a notice says that group is left out of the files ([exporting your data](export.md)). The formats are in [exporting your data](export.md).
- **Profile**, after the language, the theme and the app color: the nickname your friends see ([invitations](invitations.md)). It needs a connection.
- **Privacy**, in the account section: a ghost `Button`, "Privacy notice", that opens a `Dialog` with the notice ([ADR-0014](../adr/0014-people-in-groups.md)). The same notice opens from a ghost link on the sign-in screen, so it can be read before signing in. It takes its copy from the locales, in `en` and `es`, and says in plain words: who runs the app and how to reach them, which is Antonio López Benítez, La Coruña, España, with a link to the portfolio (`https://albz.app`) and an email link whose text is `antoniolozbez@gmail.com` and whose `mailto:` address is `antoniolozbez+tarja@gmail.com`, so the tag stays out of sight and the mail about the app can be told apart (the contact is one constant, `src/lib/contact.ts`, taken from the portfolio on 2026-11-01); what is stored, which is from Google the name, the email and the picture address, and the nickname if one was set, for each session the IP address and the browser, and what the person writes (expenses, categories, income, budgets, and the groups, the names given to members and the entries); why (to run the app, nothing else, with no ads and no tracking); who sees what (the members of a group see the name used for the person and the entries of that group, and nobody sees the email); where it lives (Cloudflare and Google's sign-in); how long (until the person deletes it; the backups D1 keeps for recovery expire on their own); and how to take it away, with the links to Your data and to Delete account. It uses one session cookie and the device's own storage for preferences and the offline copy. It is a plain statement, not legal advice: the owner has it read by someone who can before the merge.
- **Delete account**, in the account section under Sign out, as a `block` `danger` `Button`. It opens a `Dialog` with the title "Delete your account?", what goes ("your expenses, categories, income, budgets and nickname go for good", and that it can't be undone), what stays ("the shared expenses in your groups stay for the others, under 'Deleted user'"), that the backups kept for recovery expire on their own, a ghost `Button` "Download your data first", which closes the dialog and puts the focus on the heading of Your data, and a `Field` where the email of the account is typed to confirm, its hint naming the email (any case, and spaces around it, are fine: `confirmsEmail`, the server's own check). Delete is a `danger` ghost `Button`, off until the email matches, and Cancel a ghost one; the focus starts on the dialog, not on a button, and Cancel or Escape forget what was typed. While the server answers, Delete says "Deleting…", both are off and Escape doesn't close the dialog, so that a failure is always seen; a failure is said in words under the field and the person stays signed in, with different words when the server refused the email (it holds another one than the device) than when the request didn't get through. Once it is deleted the device deletes its copy and signs out, and the sign-in screen says so ([authentication](authentication.md)). It needs a connection and is off, with a notice, without one, the button and Delete in an open dialog alike.
