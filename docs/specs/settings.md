# Settings

- `/settings`, linked from the header. One column of sections, `--size-8` apart: the language, the theme (Light, Dark, then System) and the app color (each a `ChoiceGroup`, see [Color and themes](../../design-system/DESIGN.md#2-color-and-themes)), Categories (a hint and a link to `/categories`, as wide as the column), Your data, and the account (its Sign out button as wide as the column).
- **Your data** has a heading, a hint that says what the files hold and that they are made on the device, so they work offline, and two `Button`s, all your data (JSON) and only the expenses (CSV). While both fit on one line they are equal and side by side, `--size-4` apart; when they don't, they stack, each as wide as the column. A press downloads the file at once, with nothing to confirm and no state to show: the browser says that it saved it. Both are always on, offline too. The formats are in [exporting your data](export.md).
- **Profile**, after the language and the theme: the username and the handle in use ([invitations](invitations.md)). It needs a connection.
- **Delete account**, in the account section under Sign out, as a `danger` `Button`. It opens a `Dialog` with the title "Delete your account?", what goes ("your expenses, categories, income, budgets and username, for good"), what stays ("the shared expenses in your groups stay for the others, under 'Deleted user'"), a link to the backup ("Download your data first") and a `Field` where the email of the account is typed to confirm. Delete is a `danger` ghost `Button`, off until the email matches, and Cancel a ghost one; the focus starts on the dialog, not on a button. It needs a connection and is off, with a notice, without one ([authentication](authentication.md)).
