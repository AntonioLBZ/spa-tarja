# Settings

- `/settings`, linked from the header. One column of sections, `--size-8` apart: the language, the theme (Light, Dark, then System) and the app color (each a `ChoiceGroup`, see [Color and themes](../../design-system/DESIGN.md#2-color-and-themes)), Profile, Categories (a hint and a link to `/categories`, as wide as the column), Your data, and the account (its Sign out button as wide as the column).
- **Your data** has a heading, a hint that says what the files hold and that they are made on the device, so they work offline, and two `Button`s, all your data (JSON) and only the expenses (CSV). While both fit on one line they are equal and side by side, `--size-4` apart; when they don't, they stack, each as wide as the column. A press downloads the file at once, with nothing to confirm and no state to show: the browser says that it saved it. Both are on, offline too, except while the groups load for the first time: then both are off and a notice says what they wait for, and when the expenses of a group can't be had a notice says that group is left out of the files ([exporting your data](export.md)). The formats are in [exporting your data](export.md).
- **Profile**, after the language, the theme and the app color: the nickname your friends see ([invitations](invitations.md)). It needs a connection.
- **Privacy**, in the account section after Delete account: a ghost `Button`, "Privacy notice", that opens a `Dialog` with the notice ([ADR-0014](../adr/0014-people-in-groups.md)), `PrivacyNotice` in `src/features/auth`. The same button is on the sign-in screen, under the Google button, so the notice can be read before signing in ([authentication](authentication.md)). The text is in the locales (`privacy.*`, in `en` and `es`), so it is in the bundle: it opens signed out and offline and sends no request. The dialog is titled "Privacy notice" and scrolls inside when it is taller than the screen; its one action is a ghost Close, and the focus starts on the dialog and goes back to the button when it closes. It is written for a person, not a lawyer, in plain `h3` sections, short enough for a screen or two at 320 px, and it says only what the code and the specs say is true:
    - **Who is responsible**: Antonio López Benítez, La Coruña, España, and how to reach them: a link whose text is `antoniolozbez@gmail.com` and whose `mailto:` address is `antoniolozbez+tarja@gmail.com`, so the tag stays out of sight and the mail about the app can be told apart, and a link to the portfolio (`https://albz.app`). Both are ghost links, so they are as high as a button, and they are one constant (`src/lib/contact.ts`), taken from the portfolio on 2026-11-01.
    - **What is stored, and why**: only what the app needs to work. From Google the name, the email and the picture address, and never a password; the nickname if one was set; what the person writes (expenses, categories, income, budgets, groups, friends, shared expenses, settlements and the names given to people); the invitation links made (once, 14 days); for each session a cookie and the IP address and the browser it was opened from; and on the device the offline copy and the preferences, which signing out removes unless something is waiting to be sent.
    - **Who sees it**: the members of a group and the friends see what is shared in it and the name used for the person there (the nickname, else the name of the Google account); other people never see the email, and the personal expenses, categories, income and budgets are shown to nobody.
    - **Who else handles it**: Cloudflare (the hosting, the database and the platform logs of the requests, which `observability` in `wrangler.jsonc` turns on) and Google (the sign-in). No ads and no analytics, no other service gets the data, and nothing is sold.
    - **How long, and deleting the account**: until the person deletes the account; what goes (the account data, the nickname, the personal rows and the sessions, and the invitations made stop working), what stays (the shared entries, for the others, as "Deleted user" without the name) and that the backups D1 keeps for recovery expire on their own ([authentication](authentication.md)).
    - **The person's rights**: to see and download the data (Your data), to correct it (the nickname and the expenses), to delete it (Delete account), to ask by writing to the contact and to complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos. The names of the controls it points at are read from the settings' own keys, so they can't drift.
    - **Last updated**, a line with the date of the last change of the text, in each locale. A change to what the app stores, logs or sends to a third party changes the notice and that date in the same commit.

    It claims no legal compliance and is not legal advice: the owner has it read by someone who can before the merge.

- **Delete account**, in the account section under Sign out, as a `block` `danger` `Button`. It opens a `Dialog` with the title "Delete your account?", what goes ("your expenses, categories, income, budgets and nickname go for good", and that it can't be undone), what stays ("the shared expenses in your groups stay for the others, under 'Deleted user'"), that the backups kept for recovery expire on their own, a ghost `Button` "Download your data first", which closes the dialog and, once it has closed (a modal dialog keeps the rest of the page from taking the focus), puts the focus on the heading of Your data and scrolls it into view, and a `Field` where the email of the account is typed to confirm, its hint naming the email (any case, and spaces around it, are fine: `confirmsEmail`, the server's own check). Delete is a `danger` ghost `Button`, off until the email matches, and Cancel a ghost one; the focus starts on the dialog, not on a button, and Cancel or Escape forget what was typed. While the server answers, Delete says "Deleting…", both are off and Escape doesn't close the dialog, so that a failure is always seen; a failure is said in words under the field and the person stays signed in, with different words when the server refused the email (it holds another one than the device) than when the request didn't get through. Once it is deleted the device deletes its copy and signs out, and the sign-in screen says so ([authentication](authentication.md)). It needs a connection and is off, with a notice, without one, the button and Delete in an open dialog alike.
