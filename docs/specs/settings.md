# Settings

- `/settings`, linked from the header. One column of sections, `--size-8` apart: the language, the theme (Light, Dark, then System) and the app color (each a `ChoiceGroup`, see [Color and themes](../../design-system/DESIGN.md#2-color-and-themes)), Profile, Categories (a hint and a link to `/categories`, as wide as the column), Your data, and the account (its Sign out button as wide as the column).
- **Your data** has a heading, a hint that says what the files hold and that they are made on the device, so they work offline, and two `Button`s, all your data (JSON) and only the expenses (CSV). While both fit on one line they are equal and side by side, `--size-4` apart; when they don't, they stack, each as wide as the column. A press downloads the file at once, with nothing to confirm and no state to show: the browser says that it saved it. Both are on, offline too, except while the groups load for the first time: then both are off and a notice says what they wait for, and when the expenses of a group can't be had a notice says that group is left out of the files ([exporting your data](export.md)). The formats are in [exporting your data](export.md).
- **Profile**, after the language, the theme and the app color: the nickname your friends see ([invitations](invitations.md)). It needs a connection.
- **Privacy**, in the account section after Delete account: a ghost `Button`, "Privacy notice", that opens a `Dialog` with the notice ([ADR-0014](../adr/0014-people-in-groups.md)), `PrivacyNotice` in `src/features/auth`. The same button is on the sign-in screen, under the Google button and a short summary of the notice, so the notice can be read before signing in ([authentication](authentication.md)). The text is in the locales (`privacy.*`, in `en` and `es`), so it is in the bundle: it opens signed out and offline and sends no request. The dialog is titled "Privacy notice" and scrolls inside when it is taller than the screen; its one action is a ghost Close, and the focus starts on the dialog and goes back to the button when it closes. It is written for a person, not a lawyer, in plain `h3` sections, short enough for a screen or two at 320 px, and it says only what the code and the specs say is true:
    - **Who is responsible**: Antonio López Benítez, a private individual who decides what is done with the data, in La Coruña, España (Spain in English), and how to reach them: a link whose text is `antoniolozbez@gmail.com` and whose `mailto:` address is `antoniolozbez+tarja@gmail.com`, so the tag stays out of sight and the mail about the app can be told apart, and a link to the portfolio (`https://albz.app`). Both are ghost links, so they are as high as a button, and they are one constant (`src/lib/contact.ts`), taken from the portfolio on 2026-11-01.
    - **The age**, a line after the introduction: Tarja is for people aged 14 or over, the age from which a person can consent to the processing of their data in Spain. There is no check of it.
    - **What is stored, and why**: only what the app needs to work, and why: the service the person signs up for, the security of the sessions and of the request logs (a legitimate interest) and, for the names given to the people in groups, so that the group can share expenses with them. From Google, at the first sign-in, the name, the email and the number that identifies the account, and never a password; the address of the picture and the tokens Google sends are not kept, and the notice says so; the nickname if one was set, which is the name the friends see and the one the person starts with in each group made or joined; what the person writes (expenses, categories, income, budgets, groups, friends, shared expenses, settlements and the names given to the people in groups); the invitation links made (once, 14 days); for each session its cookies and the IP address and the browser it was opened from, deleted by signing out and otherwise kept until the account is deleted, since a session that is no longer used is not cleaned up; and on the device the offline copy and the preferences (language, theme, color and the grouping of the list), which go when the person signs out or the app finds out the session has ended, unless something is waiting to be sent, and also when the data of the site is cleared in the browser.
    - **Who sees it**: the members of a group and the friends see what is shared in it and the name used for the person there (the nickname, else the name of the Google account); a friend can add the person to their groups without asking, so people the person doesn't know may see that name and what is shared there; whoever opens an invitation link the person made sees the same name while the link is open; other people never see the email, and the personal expenses, categories, income and budgets are shown to nobody; and a line for the people typed as placeholders, who never agreed to anything: a first name or a nickname is enough, private details about other people are best left out, and anybody who finds out they were added by name can write to the contact and have the name taken out by hand ([authentication](authentication.md)).
    - **Who else handles it**: Cloudflare (the hosting and the database, acting only on Tarja's instructions, and the platform logs of the requests, which `observability` in `wrangler.jsonc` turns on, with technical details of the connection and kept for at most 7 days, an upper bound that holds on the free and the paid plans) and Google (the sign-in, under its own privacy policy). Both are companies based in the United States, so the data may be handled outside the European Union under the safeguards the EU accepts for such transfers (the notice does not say where the database is). No ads and no analytics, no other service gets the data, and nothing is sold.
    - **How long, and deleting the account**: until the person deletes the account; what goes (the account data, the nickname, the personal rows and the sessions, and the invitations made stop working, and the groups and friends where the person is the only one with an account, with every name and text in them), what stays (the shared entries of groups and friends somebody else has an account in, and the names of those groups and of the people in them, for the others; the person shows there as "Deleted user" without the name, and so does any name a group gave them before they joined, which is deleted too; and the friends no longer have the person among theirs) and that the backups Cloudflare keeps for recovery expire on their own, after 30 days at most, an upper bound that holds on the free and the paid plans ([authentication](authentication.md)).
    - **The person's rights**: to see and download what the person wrote and the groups they are in (Your data; the account details and the sessions are asked for by writing to the contact), to correct it (the nickname, their own name in an open group and the expenses; anything else, such as the name taken from Google, by writing to the contact), to delete it (Delete account), to ask anything, to object to a use of the data or ask to limit it by writing to the contact, with an answer within a month, and to complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es), or to the one of the EU country where the person lives. The names of the controls it points at are read from the settings' own keys, so they can't drift.
    - **Last updated**, a line with the date of the last change of the text, in each locale. A change to what the app stores, logs or sends to a third party changes the notice and that date in the same commit.

    It claims no legal compliance and is not legal advice: the owner has it read by someone who can before the merge.

- **Delete account**, in the account section under Sign out, as a `block` `danger` `Button`. It opens a `Dialog` with the title "Delete your account?", what goes ("your expenses, categories, income, budgets and nickname go for good", and so do the groups and friends where you are the only person with an account, with every name and expense in them, and that it can't be undone), what stays ("the shared expenses in your groups and with your friends stay for the others, and you show in them as 'Deleted user'", without your name or the one a group had given you before you joined, and that the friends no longer have you among theirs), that the backups kept for recovery expire on their own, after 30 days at most, a ghost `Button` "Download your data first", which closes the dialog and, once it has closed (a modal dialog keeps the rest of the page from taking the focus), puts the focus on the heading of Your data and scrolls it into view, and a `Field` where the email of the account is typed to confirm, its hint naming the email (any case, and spaces around it, are fine: `confirmsEmail`, the server's own check). Delete is a `danger` ghost `Button`, off until the email matches, and Cancel a ghost one; the focus starts on the dialog, not on a button, and Cancel or Escape forget what was typed. While the server answers, Delete says "Deleting…", both are off and Escape doesn't close the dialog, so that a failure is always seen; a failure is said in words under the field and the person stays signed in, with different words when the server refused the email (it holds another one than the device) than when the request didn't get through. Once it is deleted the device deletes its copy and signs out, and the sign-in screen says so ([authentication](authentication.md)). It needs a connection and is off, with a notice, without one, the button and Delete in an open dialog alike.
