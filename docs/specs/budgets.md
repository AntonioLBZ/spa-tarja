# Budgets

## Rules

Owner decisions (2026-10-29): a limit for the month's total and one for each category, warnings inside the app only (never a push), and a budget never blocks anything: it only warns.

- **Storage** (migration `0009`): a table `budget` with one row per user, month and scope (`user_id`, `month` as `YYYY-MM`, `scope`, `amount_cents`, `currency`, `updated_at`; the key is `(user_id, month, scope)`). `scope` is `total` or a category id; no category has the id `total` (a test checks the default ids, the custom ones are UUIDs). `amount_cents` goes from 0 to `MAX_AMOUNT_CENTS`, and **0 means no limit** from that month on.
- **The limit of a month** (`limitFor` in `core/budget.ts`, pure) is the row of the latest month up to and including it, for that scope; none, or a 0, is no limit. A month nobody set has no row of its own and holds the limit of the latest earlier month, so a limit stays until a month sets another and setting one never writes the months after it. Saving a month again replaces its row, so editing it three times is still one row. A 0 is stored, never deleted: deleting the row would make the month hold the earlier limit again. A change reaches every later month that has no row of its own. A value equal to the one the month already holds is not written, a 0 over no limit included.
- **What counts toward it** is what the summary counts: each expense adds `countedCents` to the month of `countedOn` ([when an expense counts](expense-counting.md)), the total adds all of them and a category the ones filed under it. An expense whose category no longer exists counts as "Other", as in the list. A credit card expense therefore counts in the month it is charged, and a warning says which month it speaks of: that closes the open question about a credit card whose charges pile up in a month.
- **A percentage is shown, never typed.** A limit is an amount. When the month has an income (the one the person set, without the restaurant card or what was paid back), a limit is also shown as `round(limit ÷ income × 100)` % of it, which can go over 100, and its use as `round(spent ÷ limit × 100)` %. Neither is stored.
- **Thresholds** (`core/budget.ts`): near is 80 % of the limit spent or more, over is more than the limit; spent exactly at the limit is near.
- **Warnings** come when an expense is added or duplicated, never when one is edited or deleted. For each month and scope the saved parts count in, the state before and after is compared (none, near, over): a warning comes only when a state is crossed (none to near, none to over, near to over), so being over already doesn't warn again. It also comes when the month has an income and its leftover (`summarizeMonth`) goes from zero or more to below zero. There is one message: the most serious (the leftover below zero, then over, then near), then the earliest month, then the scope with the highest use. A plan warns for the most serious of its parts. No limit, no warning.
- **API**, like the income: `GET /budgets` answers every row of the user (`month`, `scope`, `amountCents`, `currency`) and an idempotent `PUT /budgets/:month` takes `{ currency, limits: [{ scope, amountCents }] }` (1 to 200 entries, each scope once) and replaces those rows in one D1 batch, all or nothing. There is no `DELETE`. The server checks the shape of the month and of each scope (`total`, a default id or a UUID), never that a category exists, as for an expense ([categories](categories.md)). Deleting a category deletes its rows in the same batch, and the client ignores the rows of a scope it doesn't know.
- **Offline**, like the income: changing a limit needs a connection, and the `budgets` list sits in the persisted cache ([offline sync](offline-sync.md)), so the bars and the warnings work from the local copy.
- **Backup**: the JSON gains `budgets`, the rows as they are stored. The version stays 1, as nothing reads a backup yet.

## Screen

The rules are above.

- **Bars in the Summary**, between the numbers and the income form and only when the month holds a limit: the total first, then each category that has one, in the order of the category list. Each is a `Card` of two lines: the name (the badge and name of a category, "Total" for the total) with the use, a whole percentage, on the right, and under it the amounts, "€120.00 of €300.00". Over the limit, the second line adds "over by €20.00". Below the text goes a track `--size-2` tall with an ink hairline, filled in ink as wide as the use up to 100 %; over the limit the fill and the percentage turn `--color-danger`, never the only sign, since the words say it too. The bar is `aria-hidden`: the text holds everything it shows. The fill is moved with `transform` over `--duration-base`.
- **`/budgets`**, depth 2, reached from the Summary (the header underlines Summary). It has the month selector of the Summary (arrows, opens on the current month, the month is not in the URL). Under the title, one hint: a limit holds for the following months until one sets another, and 0 or an empty field means no limit from this month on. One form follows: a field for the total and one for each category (its badge and name as the label), each an amount (`Field`, as in the income form). A limit the month holds from an earlier one fills its field and says "Set in October" under it. While a field has a limit and the month has an income, a line under it reads "20 % of the income" (rounded, it can go past 100, and it is absent without an income). One Save for the whole form sends only the fields that changed, in one request, and is disabled until something does. Setting needs a connection (see Rules): offline the form is disabled, every field and the Save, and says why, and the values still show from the local copy.
- **Warnings**: when adding or duplicating an expense crosses a threshold (see Rules), a `Toast` without an action says so for 8 s and names the month: "Food of November is at 83 % of its limit", "Food of November went over its limit", "The total of November went over its limit", "This leaves November below zero". It is a `Toast` with no action (see [Components](../../design-system/DESIGN.md#components)).
