# Header

- One row on `--color-bg-secondary` with an ink hairline under it, `--size-touch` tall plus the top safe area. From 40 rem up it has three zones on a grid (`minmax(max-content, 1fr) auto minmax(max-content, 1fr)`, `--size-2` apart): Expenses on the left, the logo in the middle, and Groups, Summary and Settings on the right, `--size-4` apart so they stop reading as one phrase. The words are at `--font-size-s` and every link is `--size-touch` tall. Below 40 rem the header holds only the logo, in the middle, and the words are the bottom bar ([bottom navigation](bottom-navigation.md)).
- Expenses and the logo both lead to the home screen, with the add form adding a new expense (an edit ends): Expenses with the form collapsed, the logo with it open (see [expenses](expenses.md)). A click that opens the link somewhere else (with a modifier key, or another mouse button) leaves the form as it is.
- The word of the current section is underlined: a `--size-1` bar in ink (`--color-border`) as wide as the word, sitting on the hairline. A deeper screen underlines the one it is reached from (the charts underline Summary, the categories Settings), and that link is marked as current for assistive technology too. The logo is never underlined.
- Widths: the side columns grow alike while there is room, which keeps the logo in the middle. Nothing overflows at 640 px in either language, with the fonts loaded or not. Check it again when a word of the header or the logo changes.
- **Groups.** A group and its screens are underlined as Groups ([ADR-0012](../adr/0012-shared-expenses.md)).
