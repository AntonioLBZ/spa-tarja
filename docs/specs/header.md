# Header

The bar at the top of a screen is part of the page ([`DESIGN.md`](../../design-system/DESIGN.md#5-layout-and-mobile-first)): below 40 rem each screen draws its own, from 40 rem the root draws one header for all of them.

- **From 40 rem** the header is a band in the field color (`--color-field`), `--page-bar-height` tall, sticky at the top. It holds, left to right: the logo, the four destinations (Expenses, Groups, Summary, Settings, each a pill-shaped link of at least `--size-touch` tall, in `--color-on-field-muted`, the current one in `--color-on-field` over a pill of `--color-field-tint-strong`) and, at the far end, the "Add expense" action. A deeper screen marks the one it is reached from (the charts mark Summary, the categories Settings, a group Groups), and that link is `aria-current` for assistive technology too. The page has no bar of its own.
- **Below 40 rem** the bar is drawn by the page, sticky, in the field color, `--page-bar-height` plus the top safe area tall: the logo at the left on a tab, and on a screen deeper than a tab the link back at the left and the logo at the right. In the middle a short text, the compact title of the screen, fades in once the sheet has slid over the hero. The destinations are the tab bar ([bottom navigation](bottom-navigation.md)).
- Expenses and the logo both lead to the home screen and do nothing else: neither opens, collapses nor changes the add form, which is a sheet that the "+" opens (see [expenses](expenses.md)). The logo is never marked as current.
- **The "+" from 40 rem** ([ADR-0017](../adr/0017-the-look-of-the-plus.md), its look now in [ADR-0018](../adr/0018-the-stub-a-new-visual-identity.md)). At the end of the header: the action "Add expense" ("Añadir gasto"), a pill in the spark color (`--color-spark`, its text `--color-on-spark`), `--size-touch` tall, with `aria-haspopup="dialog"`. It opens the sheet of the add form, and inside an open group it starts the expense there ([expenses](expenses.md)). It is a button, not a link: it changes no route and is never the current one. It follows the destinations in the tab order and comes before the screen. Below 40 rem it isn't drawn: the tab bar has the "+".
- **The mark behind the current word** slides and stretches to the word of the new section as one piece, with the spring, whatever the page does under it. It is a single element with a `view-transition-name` of its own (`nav-mark`), and no script animates it. The header reads the screen already shown, not the one being opened, so the mark is still in its old place when the transition takes its first picture.
- Nothing overflows at 640 px in either language, with the fonts loaded or not. Check it again when a word of the header, the action or the logo changes.
- **Groups.** A group and its screens fall under Groups ([ADR-0012](../adr/0012-shared-expenses.md)).
- **Prerelease logo.** The app built for the prerelease ([ADR-0015](../adr/0015-github-actions-deploys-and-a-prerelease-worker.md)) writes its name as `tarja-pre`: the logo, here and on the sign-in screen, carries a `-pre` at half the size of the name, and production never does. The build knows (`CLOUDFLARE_ENV=prerelease`), so it needs no request and shows offline. The logo fits next to the widest link back (`Summary`, `Resumen`) at 320 px, and the hero-sized one fits the sign-in screen; check it again when the suffix or the name of one of those links changes.
- **The way back, below 40 rem.** An installed app on a phone has no browser back button, and a screen below a tab can otherwise be left only through the tab bar. So on every screen deeper than a tab (a group, its settings, the categories, the charts and the budgets: depth 2 or more, `backTarget` in `lib/route-transition`) the bar has a link at the left, a chevron and the name of the place it leads to. Not on the home screen, the tabs, nor `/join/$token` (depth 1).
    - **Where it leads** is the parent of the screen, not the history, so it does the same after the screen was opened from a link or the app was reopened on it: a group leads to Groups, the settings of a group to that group (the one in the address), the categories to Settings, and the charts and the budgets to Summary, the tab each is reached from. The name is the place: Groups, Group, Settings or Summary ("Grupos", "Grupo", "Ajustes", "Resumen"). The chevron is decoration (`aria-hidden`); the accessible name is "Back to Groups" ("Volver a Grupos"), which holds the words that show.
    - **Look.** A real link, the ghost `ButtonLink` ([`DESIGN.md`](../../design-system/DESIGN.md#components)) in the color written on the field, at `--font-size-s`, at least `--size-touch` tall, with the focus ring of every link. The chevron's own drawing, not its box, sits on the margin of the page. The name never wraps, and must fit at 320 px in either language beside the logo, with the fonts loaded or not; check it again when a name changes.
    - **Motion.** The parent is shallower, so leaving slides the screen back like any other way to a lower depth ([`DESIGN.md`](../../design-system/DESIGN.md#6-motion)).
- **A page that doesn't exist** (`NotFound`) is a page like the others: its bar with the logo, a title ("Page not found"), one line and a link to Expenses.
