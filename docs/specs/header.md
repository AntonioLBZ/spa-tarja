# Header

- One row on `--color-bg-secondary` with an ink hairline under it, `--size-touch` tall plus the top safe area. From 40 rem up it has three zones on a grid (`minmax(max-content, 1fr) auto minmax(max-content, 1fr)`, `--size-2` apart): Expenses and the "Add expense" action on the left, `--size-4` apart, the logo in the middle, and Groups, Summary and Settings on the right, `--size-4` apart so they stop reading as one phrase. The words are at `--font-size-s` and every link is `--size-touch` tall. Below 40 rem the header holds the logo, in the middle, and, below a tab, a link back at its left; the words are the bottom bar ([bottom navigation](bottom-navigation.md)).
- Expenses and the logo both lead to the home screen and do nothing else: neither opens, collapses nor changes the add form, which is a sheet that the "+" opens (see [expenses](expenses.md)).
- **The "+" from 40 rem** ([ADR-0017](../adr/0017-the-look-of-the-plus.md)). Right after Expenses, on the left, the header holds the text action "Add expense" ("Añadir gasto"): a ghost `Button` (the text underlined, no box, since it sits on the edge of the header and a notch there would be an L inside an L), at `--font-size-s`, `--size-touch` tall, with `aria-haspopup="dialog"`. It opens the sheet of the add form, and inside an open group it starts the expense there ([expenses](expenses.md)). It is a button, not a link: it changes no route and is never the current one. It comes before the logo in the tab order, so the keyboard reaches it ahead of the screen. It is on the left because Expenses and it, 19 characters, balance the 21 of the three words on the right; on the right the side columns would grow with a fourth word and push the logo off the middle at 640 px. Below 40 rem it isn't drawn: the bar has the "+".
- The word of the current section is underlined: a `--size-1` bar in ink (`--color-border`) as wide as the word, sitting on the hairline. A deeper screen underlines the one it is reached from (the charts underline Summary, the categories Settings), and that link is marked as current for assistive technology too. The logo is never underlined.
- Widths: the side columns grow alike while there is room, which keeps the logo in the middle. Nothing overflows at 640 px in either language, with the fonts loaded or not, and the logo stays in the middle with the action beside Expenses. Check it again when a word of the header, the action or the logo changes.
- **Hiding on scroll.** The header is sticky: it stays at the top of the screen over the content, and it slides out of sight when the person scrolls down and back in when they scroll up. It does so on a phone and from 40 rem alike: on a phone, to give the page the room, and from 40 rem, because the words and "Add expense" are then a scroll up away instead of at the very top of a long page. The bottom bar doesn't hide ([bottom navigation](bottom-navigation.md)).
    - **When.** While the page is scrolled less than the header is tall (the row, its hairline and the safe area), the header is in place, so a page that can't scroll never hides it and the top of any page shows it. Past that, it slides out once the page has gone 8 px down from the highest point it reached since the header last came in, and slides back in once the page has gone 8 px up from the lowest point since it went out: the 8 px are counted from where the movement began, so a shaking hand doesn't flicker it. The scroll position is held between the top and the end of the page before it is read, so the rubber-band overscroll of iOS at either end, going out and coming back, changes nothing. Pressing the current tab of the bar, which scrolls to the top, shows it.
    - **Motion.** A `translateY` of its own height over `--duration-base` with `--ease-standard`, `transform` only ([`DESIGN.md`](../../design-system/DESIGN.md#6-motion)); with reduced motion the durations are zero and it just changes. The header keeps its place in the page while it is out (sticky takes no room from the content), so hiding it moves nothing and cannot feed back into the scroll position.
    - **Out of sight, not out of the page.** It is never `display: none`, `visibility: hidden`, `inert` or `aria-hidden`: its links stay in the tab order and in the accessibility tree, so Shift+Tab from the content reaches them and a screen reader finds the same banner. A control in it that takes the focus from the keyboard (`:focus-visible`) shows the header and keeps it shown until the focus leaves it, so the ring is never off screen; a click doesn't, so a header you pressed with the mouse hides again like any other.
    - **Under it.** `scroll-padding-top` on the page is `--header-height` (the row of the header and its hairline) plus the top safe area, the header's own height, so what is brought into view (Tab, `scrollIntoView`, an address with a fragment) stops under the header and not behind it.
    - **The notch and the toolbar.** The top safe area is part of the header (its padding), so a hidden header would show the page under the status bar of a phone that draws the page there. A strip of the header's color, as tall as `env(safe-area-inset-top)` and fixed to the top, stays when the header is out; it has no height where there is no inset. The browser toolbar (`theme-color`) mirrors the header's surface, not whether the header is on screen, and doesn't change with the scroll ([`DESIGN.md`](../../design-system/DESIGN.md#2-color-and-themes)).
    - **Shadow under it** ([ADR-0018](../adr/0018-a-shadow-where-content-scrolls-under-an-edge.md)). While content is scrolled under the header, it casts a soft shadow (`--color-shadow`) on it, under the hairline: nothing at the top of the page, full after `--size-6` of scroll. A pseudo-element of the header holds it and only its `opacity` follows the scroll (scroll-driven animation, no script); where the browser lacks it there is no shadow. It slides with the header.
    - **Open decisions, with the default taken.** Both widths (the alternative: only on a phone, with a static header on desktop); the bottom bar always in place (the alternative: it slides down with the header and comes back with it); the 8 px (a larger one is calmer, a smaller one quicker).
- **Groups.** A group and its screens are underlined as Groups ([ADR-0012](../adr/0012-shared-expenses.md)).
- **Prerelease logo.** The app built for the prerelease ([ADR-0015](../adr/0015-github-actions-deploys-and-a-prerelease-worker.md)) writes its name as `Tarja-pre`: the logo, here and on the sign-in screen, carries a `-pre` at half the size of the name, in the body font ([`DESIGN.md`](../../design-system/DESIGN.md)), and production never does. The build knows (`CLOUDFLARE_ENV=prerelease`), so it needs no request and shows offline. The logo stays in the middle at 320 px in either language, also next to the widest link back (`Summary`, `Resumen`), and the hero-sized one fits the sign-in screen; check it again when the suffix or the name of one of those links changes.
- **The way back, below 40 rem.** An installed app on a phone has no browser back button, and a screen below a tab can otherwise be left only through the bottom bar. So on every screen deeper than a tab (a group, its settings, the categories, the charts and the budgets: depth 2 or more, `backTarget` in `lib/route-transition`) the header has a link at the left, a chevron and the name of the place it leads to. Not on the home screen, the tabs, nor `/join/$token` (depth 1). The drawing of the chevron, not its box, sits on the margin of the page: the link is pulled back by the empty third of the icon. From 40 rem up it isn't drawn: the header has the words, and the browser its arrow.
    - **Where it leads** is the parent of the screen, not the history, so it does the same after the screen was opened from a link or the app was reopened on it: a group leads to Groups, the settings of a group to that group (the one in the address), the categories to Settings, and the charts and the budgets to Summary, the tab each is reached from. The name is the place: Groups, Group, Settings or Summary ("Grupos", "Grupo", "Ajustes", "Resumen"). The chevron is decoration (`aria-hidden`); the accessible name is "Back to Groups" ("Volver a Grupos"), which holds the words that show.
    - **Look.** A real link, the ghost `ButtonLink` ([`DESIGN.md`](../../design-system/DESIGN.md#components)): the name underlined, at `--font-size-s`, at least `--size-touch` tall and wide, with the focus ring of every link. It sits on the left column of the grid the header has on a phone, `minmax(max-content, 1fr) auto minmax(max-content, 1fr)`, so the logo stays in the middle with or without it. The name never wraps and must fit the left column at 320 px in either language, with the fonts loaded or not, without pushing the logo off the middle; check it again when a name changes.
    - **Motion.** The parent is shallower, so leaving slides the screen back like any other way to a lower depth ([`DESIGN.md`](../../design-system/DESIGN.md#6-motion)). The link comes and goes with the header's own cross-fade, as the header reads the screen already shown.
