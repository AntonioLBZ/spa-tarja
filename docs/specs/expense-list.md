# The expense list

## Rules

- **The list is by the day made, not by what counts.** The expense list (`core/expense-list.ts`) filters, groups and adds up by `date`: a search or a date range looks at the day made, week and month groups are those of the day made (weeks start on Monday), and the subtotal on each group adds what its rows show, a plan once at its full price on its purchase day (`planTotal`) and a restaurant card expense at your part (`countedCents`). A credit card expense or a part of a plan is therefore never moved to the day it is charged there, so a subtotal is not what was spent in that period and can differ from the summary and the charts, which read `countedOn`. Everything is computed on the device from the local expenses, so it works offline and sees what is not synced yet.
- `buildList(expenses, filters, grouping, ctx)` is a pure function of the expenses held on the device. It drops the deleted rows, sorts newest first (the day made, then `createdAt`), collapses the parts of a plan into one row, filters on the row shown (a plan's first part) and groups the rows by day, week, month or category. Each group carries its subtotal. The collapse comes first so that a plan is searched, filtered and counted once, and an edit or a delete always gets every part of the plan, never a filtered copy.
- **Filters**: the text (every normalised word of the search is a substring of the description plus the name of the category as the row shows it; `normalizeText` in `core/text.ts` lowercases and strips accents, as the classifier does), the category, a range on the day made with both ends included and either one optional, and how it was paid (any, now, credit card, restaurant card, installments). An expense whose category no longer exists counts as the fallback category inside the filter and the grouping only; the expense itself is not rewritten ([categories](categories.md)).
- **Groups**: days, weeks (from Monday) and months newest first, categories by subtotal from the biggest and by id on a tie.
- The filters are the screen's state and are lost on leaving it, so a stale filter cannot hide a new expense. The grouping is a preference kept in `localStorage` as `tarja:list-grouping`. The search is deferred (`useDeferredValue`) and the list draws 100 rows at a time, with "Show more", rather than windowing it: the cards vary in height, a deleted row fades out before it goes and the browser's find in page has to see the rows.
- `buildList` is `listItems` (drop, sort, collapse: depends on the expenses only, so it runs once per change of data) followed by `groupItems` (filter and group: runs on each keystroke, with the text a search looks in built once per expense), and `limitRows` cuts the groups at the rows to draw while each keeps its whole subtotal.

## Screen

- Under the title of the list, once there is an expense, come a search field, a "Filters" toggle and, while anything is narrowing the list, a status line. With no expenses at all there are no controls, only "No expenses yet".
- **Search** is a full-width `type="search"` field inside a `Field`. Every word typed has to appear in the description or in the name of the category as the row shows it, ignoring case and accents, so "caf" finds "Café" and "other" finds an expense whose category was deleted. It narrows the list as the person types, deferred so typing never waits for the list. The × that clears it is ours, not the blue one Chrome and Safari draw: the `close` icon (see [Icons](../../design-system/DESIGN.md#icons)) in `--color-content-primary`, inside a `--size-touch` square, shown by the browser only while there is text. It clears the search alone; the Clear button of the status line below drops the filters too.
- **Filters (n)** is a `Disclosure` closed by default, with the chevron; `n` is how many of the filters in its panel are in use (category, dates and paid with count one each; the grouping is not a filter), and it is left out at zero. The panel comes in like any other, with a fade and a short vertical shift (see [Motion](../../design-system/DESIGN.md#6-motion)), and holds, in this order and `--size-4` apart: Group by (a `ChoiceGroup`: Day, Week, Month, Category, whose options wrap as those of any `ChoiceGroup` do: at 320 px, Day and Week, then Month, then Category), Filter by category (the `CategorySelect` of the charts, starting at all of them; its label differs from the "Category" of the add form so the two are never mixed up), From and To (`DateRangeFields`, either end may be empty), and Paid with (a `ChoiceGroup`: Any, Now, Credit card, Restaurant card, Installments).
- The filters look at the row as it is shown. A plan is one row: the search and the category look at its description and category, the dates at its purchase day, and Installments keeps the plans while Now keeps what was paid at once and is not part of one. Dates are those of the day the expense was made, both ends included. A category that no longer exists counts as "Other" for the filter, and the expense keeps its id.
- **The status line** is a quiet `Notice` and a Clear button, shown while the search or any filter is in use: "Showing x of y", or "No expenses match" when nothing does. The `Notice` sits in a `role="status"` region that is always in the page and empty while nothing narrows the list, so that the first count is announced as well as the next ones. Clear drops the search and every filter, leaves the grouping alone and puts the focus on the search field, since the button is gone.
- **What is remembered.** The filters live in the screen's state only: leaving the screen or reloading clears them, so a filter left on never hides a new expense. For the same reason, adding an expense from the form clears the search and every filter (not the grouping), and so does the list having no expenses left; rows that arrive from the server never clear them, as the person may be typing. The grouping is a preference like the theme and is kept in `localStorage` (`tarja:list-grouping`; a blocked storage means it lasts for the session).
- **Groups.** A group's header has the label at the start and the subtotal at the end, over the same hairline as before. The day is the long weekday date; a week reads "Week of" its Monday; a month is its name; a category is its icon and name. The year is added to the label when the groups cross a New Year. Days, weeks and months go newest first, and the rows inside a group too; categories go from the biggest subtotal to the smallest, and by id when they tie. A screen reader hears "Total" before the subtotal. When the grouping is not by day, each row's details start with the day it was made.
- **The subtotal** adds the rows of the group, all of them, even those still behind "Show more": a plan at its full price on its purchase day and a restaurant card expense at your part. It is by the day made, so it is not what was spent in that period, and it can differ from the summary and the charts, which count each expense on the day it is charged ([when an expense counts](expense-counting.md)).
- **Show more.** The list draws 100 rows, and "Show more" under it adds 100 each time; any change to the search, a filter or the grouping starts over at 100. The focus goes to the first row that "Show more" brings in, also when that was the last press and the button is gone. Variable-height cards, the fade of a deleted row and the browser's find in page rule out windowing the list.
- On a phone the search field and the toggle take the full width, and nothing overflows down to 320 px: the dates stack under 365 px or so, and the options of a `ChoiceGroup` wrap.
