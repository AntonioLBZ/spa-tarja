# The expense list

## Rules

- **The list is by the day made, not by what counts.** The expense list (`core/expense-list.ts`) filters, groups and adds up by `date`: a search or a date range looks at the day made, week and month groups are those of the day made (weeks start on Monday), and the subtotal on each group adds what its rows show, a plan once at its full price on its purchase day (`planTotal`) and a restaurant card expense at your part (`countedCents`). A credit card expense or a part of a plan is therefore never moved to the day it is charged there, so a subtotal is not what was spent in that period and can differ from the summary and the charts, which read `countedOn`. Everything is computed on the device from the local expenses, so it works offline and sees what is not synced yet.
- `buildList(expenses, filters, grouping, ctx)` is a pure function of the expenses held on the device. It drops the deleted rows, sorts newest first (the day made, then `createdAt`), collapses the parts of a plan into one row, filters on the row shown (a plan's first part) and groups the rows by day, week, month or category. Each group carries its subtotal. The collapse comes first so that a plan is searched, filtered and counted once, and an edit or a delete always gets every part of the plan, never a filtered copy.
- **Filters**: the text (every normalised word of the search is a substring of the description plus the name of the category as the row shows it, and, for a shared row, the name of its group or friend (`groupNameOf`); `normalizeText` in `core/text.ts` lowercases and strips accents, as the classifier does), the category, a range on the day made with both ends included and either one optional, how it was paid (any, now, credit card, restaurant card, installments) and who it is shared with (below). An expense whose category no longer exists counts as the fallback category inside the filter and the grouping only; the expense itself is not rewritten ([categories](categories.md)).
- **The list opens on this month**: From starts as the 1st of the current month and To empty (`openingFilters`), so the list shows what was made this month, and anything dated later, not everything ever logged. That range is the list's own state, not a filter the person chose: it does not count in the number of the Filters button and does not bring the status line. Any other dates count as one filter, unless both are empty, since then nothing narrows the list. Clear empties both. When an expense is added the dates go back to that range, or, if the day of the new expense is in another month, to the first and the last day of that month (`filtersAfterAdding`), so the new expense is always in the list.
- **Groups**: days, weeks (from Monday) and months newest first, categories by subtotal from the biggest and by id on a tie.
- The filters are the screen's state and are lost on leaving it, so a stale filter cannot hide a new expense. The grouping is a preference kept in `localStorage` as `tarja:list-grouping`. The search is deferred (`useDeferredValue`) and the list draws 100 rows at a time, with "Show more", rather than windowing it: the cards vary in height, a deleted row fades out before it goes and the browser's find in page has to see the rows.
- `buildList` is `listItems` (drop, sort, collapse: depends on the expenses only, so it runs once per change of data) followed by `groupItems` (filter and group: runs on each keystroke, with the text a search looks in built once per expense), and `limitRows` cuts the groups at the rows to draw while each keeps its whole subtotal.

## Screen

- **Pinned groups** ([ADR-0021](../adr/0021-pinned-groups.md)). While the person has pinned groups or friends ([groups](groups.md)), the title of the screen is a row of tabs, a `nav` named "Expenses and pinned groups": Expenses first, then the pinned ones that are open, in the order they were pinned, each named as in Groups (the other person's name for a friend). Each tab is a link to this screen, a pinned one with the group in the address (`/?group=<id>`), and the current one carries `aria-current="page"`. A tab shows the group as its own screen does ([groups](groups.md)), with its name as a heading that only assistive technology reads, since the tab says it, and while it is shown the "+" starts an expense in that group, as inside the group. The row never wraps: Expenses keeps its width, the others share the rest and a long name is cut with an ellipsis (the whole name is the link's `title`). The words are at `--font-size-m` and bold, in secondary text but the current one, in ink with a bar of `--size-1` in ink under its word, as the current word of the header; no notch and no rule under the row. Each tab is `--size-touch` tall, the tabs `--size-4` apart. An address with a group that isn't pinned and open for the person shows Expenses, so a tab that goes away (unpinned, or the group closed) falls back to it. With nothing pinned the title is the plain heading "Expenses" as before. The tabs work offline, from the copy on the device. Changing tab slides the screen under the row along it, as the sections slide along the bottom bar, while the row holds still ([`DESIGN.md`](../../design-system/DESIGN.md#6-motion)).
- Under the title of the list, once there is an expense, come a row with the search field and the Filters button, and, while anything is narrowing the list, a status line. With no expenses at all there are no controls, only "No expenses yet".
- **Search** is a `type="search"` field inside a `Field`, taking the row but the button, with the `search` icon (a magnifying glass) inside the box, before its floating label and its text ([ADR-0023](../adr/0023-buttons-and-fields-draw-their-notch-as-used.md)). Every word typed has to appear in the description, in the name of the category as the row shows it or, for a shared row, in the name of its group or friend, ignoring case and accents, so "caf" finds "Café" and "other" finds an expense whose category was deleted. It narrows the list as the person types, deferred so typing never waits for the list. The × that clears it is ours, not the blue one Chrome and Safari draw: the `close` icon (see [Icons](../../design-system/DESIGN.md#icons)) in `--color-content-primary`, inside a `--size-touch` square in the middle of the box, still like the magnifying glass, shown by the browser only while there is text. It clears the search alone; the Clear button of the status line below drops the filters too.
- **The Filters button** ([ADR-0024](../adr/0024-the-list-filters-open-in-a-sheet.md)) is a ghost icon button at the right of the search field, in the middle of the field's box, which holds the label (ADR-0023): the `filter_list` icon in a `--size-touch` square. It is named "Filters", or "Filters (n)" where `n` is how many of the filters of the sheet are in use (category, dates, paid with and shared with count one each, the dates only when they are not the range the list opens on; the grouping is not a filter), and while `n` is not zero it shows the number, small and bold, in its top right corner, out of the flow, so the icon never moves. It says it opens a dialog (`aria-haspopup="dialog"`).
- **The filters sheet** is the `sheet` variant of `Dialog`, titled "Filters": from the bottom on a phone, a centered dialog from 40 rem. It holds, in this order and `--size-4` apart: Group by (a `ChoiceGroup`: Day, Week, Month, Category, as many to a row as fit, like the options of any `ChoiceGroup`), Filter by category (the `CategorySelect` of the charts, starting at all of them; its label differs from the "Category" of the add form so the two are never mixed up), From and To (`DateRangeFields`, starting on the 1st of this month and empty; either end may be empty), Paid with (a `ChoiceGroup`: Any, Now, Credit card, Restaurant card, Installments; on a narrow phone the longest word may not fit twice in a row, and then they go one under the other), Shared with (a `Select`, below) and, while anything narrows the list, a quiet `Notice` in a `role="status"` region with the same words as the status line ("Showing x of y" or "No expenses match"), since the list is behind the sheet. Every choice applies at once. Its actions are stuck to its foot: "Clear filters" at the left, which drops the filters of the sheet, the dates too, and leaves the search and the grouping alone (disabled while no filter is on), and "Close" at the right. Escape and a tap outside close it too; it has no × and no Done. The focus starts on the dialog and goes back to the button when it closes. The sheet stays in the page while closed, as every `Dialog` does.
- The filters look at the row as it is shown. A plan is one row: the search and the category look at its description and category, the dates at its purchase day, and Installments keeps the plans while Now keeps what was paid at once and is not part of one. Dates are those of the day the expense was made, both ends included. A category that no longer exists counts as "Other" for the filter, and the expense keeps its id.
- **The status line** is a quiet `Notice` and a Clear button, shown while the search or any filter is in use, the dates only when they are not the range the list opens on: "Showing x of y", or "No expenses match" when nothing does. The `Notice` sits in a `role="status"` region that is always in the page and empty while nothing narrows the list, so that the first count is announced as well as the next ones. Clear is a ghost `Button` at the right of the `Notice`, on the same row and level with its text, since a box under it would weigh more than the line it goes with. Pressing it drops the search and every filter, the dates too, so the list shows every expense; it leaves the grouping alone and puts the focus on the filters button next to the search, since the Clear button is gone: never on the search field, which would open the keyboard of a phone.
- **What is remembered.** The filters live in the screen's state only: leaving the screen or reloading puts them back as they were on opening, so a filter left on never hides a new expense. For the same reason, adding an expense from the form clears the search and every filter but the dates, which take the range described above (not the grouping), and the list having no expenses left clears all of them; rows that arrive from the server never clear them, as the person may be typing. The grouping is a preference like the theme and is kept in `localStorage` (`tarja:list-grouping`; a blocked storage means it lasts for the session).
- **Groups.** Each group is a ticket ([the ticket](../../design-system/DESIGN.md#3-shape)): the label on top, the rows and, at the foot, the line "Total" with the subtotal, whatever the grouping is. The day is the long weekday date; a week reads "Week of" its Monday; a month is its name; a category is its icon and name. The year is added to the label when the groups cross a New Year. Days, weeks and months go newest first, and the rows inside a group too; categories go from the biggest subtotal to the smallest, and by id when they tie. The foot says "Total" in visible text, so a screen reader hears it before the subtotal. When the grouping is not by day, each row's details start with the day it was made.
- **The subtotal** adds the rows of the group, all of them, even those still behind "Show more": a plan at its full price on its purchase day and a restaurant card expense at your part. It is by the day made, so it is not what was spent in that period, and it can differ from the summary and the charts, which count each expense on the day it is charged ([when an expense counts](expense-counting.md)).
- **Show more.** The list draws 100 rows, and "Show more" under it adds 100 each time; any change to the search, a filter or the grouping starts over at 100. The focus goes to the first row that "Show more" brings in, also when that was the last press and the button is gone. Variable-height cards, the fade of a deleted row and the browser's find in page rule out windowing the list.
- On a phone the search field and the button share the full width, and nothing overflows down to 320 px: the dates stack under 365 px or so, and the options of a `ChoiceGroup` go as many to a row as the widest word of a label allows, a long label going on to a second line, down to one to a row.

## Shared expenses

The list also shows your part of the expenses of your groups ([shared expenses](shared-expenses.md)), as rows like any other: the day made, your part as the amount and the same grouping, filters and subtotals, which add your part. They are paid now for everybody but the payer, who may have used a credit or restaurant card, so they are found under those filters too. The details of a row say the group (the person, for a friend), who paid ("You paid" when it was you) and, when it isn't your part, the total: "Trip to Lisbon · Ana paid €100.00", and, for your own card, how and when it is charged, as for an expense of yours. The search also looks in the name of the group or of the person. **Shared with** is one more filter, a `Select` after Paid with: Anyone (the start), Just me, and each group and friend whose rows can be in the list (Show in my expenses on, closed ones too), the groups first, by the names and icons of With in the add form; it counts as one filter. Just me keeps the person's own expenses, plans included, and a group or friend keeps its shared rows (`sharedWith` in `core/expense-list.ts`, `JUST_ME` or the group's id, read through `isSharedRow`). The panel of a shared row holds Edit, Duplicate and Delete, which act on the entry of the group for every member, and the toast of a delete says from which group ([shared expenses](shared-expenses.md)): Edit and Duplicate open the add form at home on that entry, with the group fixed, and an entry that names somebody who left can't be edited there either, with the same quiet notice as in its ledger, while Duplicate leaves that person out. A delete or an Undo the device refuses (the group closed meanwhile) is said under the title of the list; the row of a delete is kept as it was until the delete is safe on the device, and only then fades out, takes the focus away and brings the toast. A shared row of a closed group has nothing to open, as in its ledger: the entry is read-only. A shared row never forms a plan.
