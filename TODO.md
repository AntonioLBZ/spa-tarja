- Installments: an expense paid in several monthly parts, like PayPal's pay in 3. The app splits the total evenly in cents (the remainder goes to the first parts) and the user can change the amount of each month. While the parts don't add up to the total, the form shows the difference ("Remaining 1 €" for 15 € in 3 months entered as 5-5-4) and doesn't let the user save; it also refuses parts that go over the total. Each part counts in its own month.
