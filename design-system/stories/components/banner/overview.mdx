# Banner

The notice of something that happened elsewhere, a shared expense somebody added, shaped like the banner of a system notification. It drops in from the top, over the header: a title and a message that together open what it is about, and a button that clears it away.

## Usage

<Canvas of={Examples.Basic} sourceState="shown" />

`BannerProvider` goes once above the routes, so a banner survives a change of screen, and `useBanner` gives `show` and `dismiss`. One at a time: what `show` gets while one shows replaces its text and starts its count again. It goes by itself after 8 s, held while the pointer is over it or the focus inside; a swipe to a side or up, or Escape, clears it. It never takes the focus, and its `role="status"` region is always in the page, so what comes in is announced. Here the frame stands for the page, since the banner sits on its top edge.
