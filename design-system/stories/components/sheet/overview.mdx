# Sheet

A sheet of paper, like a receipt: `--color-bg-secondary`, closed by a double rule, under a label centered in capitals over a dashed rule. The label is an `h2`, or an `h3` with `level`.

## Usage

<Canvas of={Examples.Basic} sourceState="shown" />

What goes on it is up to the screen: the expenses of a day with their total, the categories, the balances and the ledger of a group. A `Disclosure` on a sheet has no notch on its toggle. `labelRef` lets the screen put the focus on the label when the row that had it is gone, as a person removed from a group.

## Without a label

Neither the heading nor the dashed rule: the ledger of a group in the order the entries were added.

<Canvas of={Examples.NoLabel} />

## With an action

A small ghost button at the end of the row of the label, the label staying centered: Settle up on the balances of a group.
