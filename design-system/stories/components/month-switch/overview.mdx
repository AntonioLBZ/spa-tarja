# MonthSwitch

The name of a month between two arrow buttons, previous and next, its first letter in capital. The name is a `role="status"`, always in the page, so the new month is read after a press.

## Usage

<Canvas of={Examples.Basic} sourceState="shown" />

For the screens that show one month at a time, the Summary and the budgets. The month is the state of the screen, never part of the URL. `labels` names the two buttons.
