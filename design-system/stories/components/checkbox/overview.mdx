# Checkbox

A square box with its label, for a yes or no. The box has the notch and sits on `--color-bg-secondary` like a field; checked, it fills with the accent and shows a check. The whole row is the target, and a native checkbox under the box keeps the keyboard, the focus ring and the screen reader.

## Usage

<Canvas of={Examples.Basic} sourceState="shown" />

A switch of the settings or of a group, and who is in a shared expense. Several in a list are `--size-2` apart. A choice among several is a `ChoiceGroup` or a `Select`.
