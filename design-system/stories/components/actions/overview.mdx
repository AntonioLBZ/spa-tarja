# Actions

A row of actions, buttons or links, `--size-4` apart, that wraps onto as many lines as it needs. It draws nothing of its own: the look is the actions'.

## Usage

<Canvas of={Examples.Basic} sourceState="shown" />

Use it for the actions under an open expense, a category or a person, the foot of a `Dialog` and the choices of a screen. A row whose boxes share its width, like Settle up, is not an `Actions`.

## With links

The same row holds links, as the links to Budgets and Charts at the foot of Summary.
