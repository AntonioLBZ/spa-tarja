# Button

A box with the notch drawn as it is used: at rest only its bottom edge, the left edge growing to half the height on hover and to the whole height while pressed, which makes the L. On a touch screen a button at rest is its bottom line alone.

## Usage

<Canvas of={Examples.Basic} sourceState="shown" />

Every action of the app that isn't a link to a screen. It is `type="button"` unless it submits. A link that looks like a button is a `ButtonLink`; a control with a look of its own is a raw `<button>`.

## Primary

The accent fill, for the main action of a form.

<Canvas of={Examples.Primary} />

## Danger

Both edges and the text in `--color-danger`, for what deletes.

<Canvas of={Examples.Danger} />

## Ghost

The text alone, underlined, with no box and no padding at the sides, for an action inside another edge or on paper: the actions under an open expense, the Undo of a toast. It goes with the default and `danger` variants, never with `primary` or `pressed`.

<Canvas of={Examples.Ghost} />

## Block

As wide as its row, for a button that stands alone in its section, so its bottom edge runs from side to side.

<Canvas of={Examples.Block} />

## Small

The text in `--font-size-s`, for the actions among the words of the header.

<Canvas of={Examples.Small} />

## Glyph

A box that holds only a symbol, centered; it needs an `aria-label`. A `badge` puts a short mark in its corner, hidden from assistive technology, so the label says it too: the number of filters on.

<Canvas of={Examples.Glyph} />

## Pressed

A toggle that is on: filled with the accent, keeping the whole L, with `aria-pressed`.

<Canvas of={Examples.Pressed} />

## Disabled

The same whatever the variant: no fill, secondary text and the notch in the hairline color.

<Canvas of={Examples.Disabled} />
