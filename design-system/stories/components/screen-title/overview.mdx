# ScreenTitle

The `h1` of a screen, the one place that names it. It sets the title of the page to "&lt;heading&gt; – Tarja", and when its screen has just opened it takes the focus, with no ring and no scroll, or says its text through the `Announcer`, as the way the screen was reached asks. Nothing happens at the first load.

## Usage

<Canvas of={Examples.Basic} sourceState="shown" />

Every screen has one, and never a bare `<h1>`; the brand is not a heading. A screen reached from a `nav`, by a swipe or through the history keeps the focus where it was and says the screen; any other change moves the focus to the title.
