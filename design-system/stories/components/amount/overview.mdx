# Amount

Money as the app writes it, with up to three cues of which way it goes from the side of whoever its line is about: the amount colors, a sign and an arrow. Digits are tabular and the amount never wraps.

## Usage

<Canvas of={Examples.Basic} sourceState="shown" />

Use it for money that goes one way or the other: the balances and the ledger of a group, the Left of the summary and the sentences of a balance. The screen says which cues it wants and the person's settings say which they allow (`useAmount`); the component reads no setting itself. Never in the list of expenses, the budgets or the charts, where every amount goes the same way.

## With an arrow

The arrow goes up for what adds to a balance and down for what takes from it, at the size of the text. It shows only in the balances of a group.

<Canvas of={Examples.Arrow} />

## Color alone

In a sentence the words already say which way it goes, so the amount keeps only its color.

<Canvas of={Examples.ColorAlone} />

## No direction

Without a direction no cue shows, whatever is asked: a settlement in the ledger of a group.

<Canvas of={Examples.Neutral} />

## The palette for color blindness

A blue and an orange instead of the green and the red, picked in Settings → Amounts. The app sets `data-money-palette="color-blind"` on `<html>`; the example sets it around each amount.
