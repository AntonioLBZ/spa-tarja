# Field

A box that holds the label and the control and, under it, a hint and the error, tied together for assistive technology. The box draws its notch as it is used: at rest its bottom edge alone, the left edge growing to half the height on hover and to the whole height while it has the focus. The label floats: in the middle of the box while the field is empty and idle, small at its top once it has the focus or a value.

## Usage

<Canvas of={Examples.Basic} sourceState="shown" />

Every text field of the app, and a `Select`. The control is a function of `control`, which it spreads on its element: the `id` the label points at, a blank placeholder, `aria-describedby` and `aria-invalid`. Fields in a form are `--size-4` apart. A `ChoiceGroup` has its own legend and stays outside.
