# Disclosure

A toggle and the panel it opens under it, with `aria-expanded` on the toggle. The toggle draws its notch as it is used: closed, its bottom edge alone; the left edge grows to half the height on hover and to the whole height while pressed and while open. The panel comes in with a fade and a short shift.

## Usage

<Canvas of={Examples.Basic} sourceState="shown" />

What can stay folded until somebody asks for it: the numbers of a chart, the closed and archived groups, the rows that open on a `Sheet` (which keep their own look). It keeps its own state from `defaultOpen`, or is controlled with `open` and `onOpenChange`. `keepMounted` keeps a closed panel in the page, hidden, so what was typed in it survives. `chevron` ends the toggle with an arrow that turns half a turn when open.
