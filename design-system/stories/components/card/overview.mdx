# Card

A row drawn as its bottom edge alone, a thick ink line, with the padding of a row above and below and none at the sides. It has no left edge and no L, at rest or on hover, since a card isn't a control.

## Usage

<Canvas of={Examples.Basic} sourceState="shown" />

A row of a list: a budget, a category of the charts, a group. What goes inside it is up to the screen. Several go in a `CardList`; a row that opens is a `Disclosure` on a `Sheet` instead. A `Button` in a card sits into its padding, so the row is no taller than the touch target.

## Linked

Its one link covers the whole card, and the focus ring of the link goes round the card: the pages of the settings, the groups.
