# DateRangeFields

The From and To fields of a range of days, each a `Field`, side by side while both fit and one under the other on a narrow phone. A day counts only between 2000 and 2099: what doesn't make a day yet, as a year being typed, stays in the field without reaching the screen, and leaving the field puts the last good day back.

<Canvas of={Examples.Basic} sourceState="shown" />

The range of the charts and the From and To of the list filters, of a group's ledger and of the printable views. `onChange` gets the end that changed and its day.
