# LiveNotice

A `Notice` that comes and goes, inside a `role="status"` region that is always in the page. A live region that arrives already filled is announced inconsistently, so only what is inside it changes. Empty, it takes no room.

## Usage

<Canvas of={Examples.Basic} sourceState="shown" />

A line that comes and goes while the person looks at it: being offline, a failed load, a copied link. Its children are the message, or nothing while there is none to say. A message that is on screen from its first paint is a plain `Notice`; one with no place on screen is said through the `Announcer`. It takes the `tone` of a `Notice`.
