# Motion

Things enter, leave and change with a transition: it is part of how the app looks. Every transition takes a duration and an easing from the tokens, and reduced motion turns them all off in one place.

## Usage

The edge that grows on a `Button`:

```css
.button:not(.button--ghost)::before {
    transform: scaleY(0);
    transform-origin: bottom;
    transition: transform var(--duration-base) var(--ease-standard);
}
```

A transition always takes a `--duration-*` and an `--ease-*`, never a literal value, and animates `transform` and `opacity` where it can. With `prefers-reduced-motion` the durations are 0, so a component never checks it itself: what moved just changes.

## Durations

`fast`, `base` and `slow` for the interface; `reveal` only for the change of theme. Press Play to move them all, with `--ease-standard`.

## Easings

`standard` for what moves or changes in place, `enter` for what comes in and `exit` for what leaves. Press Play to move them all over `--duration-slow`.
