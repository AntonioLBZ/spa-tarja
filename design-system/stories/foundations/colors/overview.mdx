# Colors

Flat colors only, no gradients and no blur: white or a dark gray (true black with the OLED tone), text in the opposite one, neutral grays, one accent (a gray), one danger color, the two amount colors and the category palette. Each token is defined once with `light-dark()`, so it holds both themes.

## Usage

The quiet `Notice`:

```css
.notice--quiet {
    border-left-color: var(--color-border-subtle);
    color: var(--color-content-secondary);
}
```

A component takes only the semantic `--color-*` tokens, never a raw value: those live in `tokens.css`. Meaning never rests on color alone, so a color always comes with words, an icon or a place. A new color needs a reason and a row in the palette table of `DESIGN.md`. In forced colors a component says again with system colors what it draws only with a background, in a block at the end of its own CSS.

## Dark tones

The dark theme comes in two tones: a dark gray by default, and pure black for OLED screens, which the person turns on in Settings → Appearance. Only the surfaces and the hairline change, through these three tokens that the dark side of their `--color-*` reads; `data-dark-tone="oled"` on `<html>` swaps in the black ones. The Dark tone toolbar shows a story in either.

## Accent, danger and shadow

The accent is the hue of the interface: links, selection and the primary action. Danger is for errors and what deletes, nothing else; there are no success or warning colors. The shadow is the color of `--shadow-popup`, the only shadow, for what opens over the page and where content scrolls under an edge.

## Amounts

Which way an amount goes, from the side of whoever its line is about: they paint the text of the amount, its sign and its arrow, and nothing else. Owing is not an error, so they are tokens of their own, apart from danger.

<Canvas of={Examples.Amounts} sourceState="none" />

The person can pick the palette for color blindness in Settings → Amounts, a blue and an orange that `data-money-palette="color-blind"` swaps in.

## Categories

They help find a category at a glance and paint its icon and its part of a chart, never text, borders or a fill behind text. The database keeps the key (`vermillion`), never the value.
