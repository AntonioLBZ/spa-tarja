# LedgerRow

An entry of a group's ledger, a line of the ticket like an expense of the person's list: its name, what it does to their balance in the group as an `Amount` with color and sign, and under them who paid the whole, or that it is theirs alone or not theirs. Pressing it opens who paid and each share, who wrote it last when it wasn't the person, and Edit, Duplicate and Delete.

## Usage

<Canvas of={Examples.Basic} sourceState="shown" />

One per entry in the `CardList` of a day on its `Sheet`, in the group's ledger. Its amount is what the entry does to the person's balance: here Marta paid 60 and her part was 30, so she gets 30 back.
