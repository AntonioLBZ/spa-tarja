# ExpenseRow

An expense of the list, a line of the ticket of its day: its category badge, its text and its amount, and under them a quiet line with its category, how it was paid and when it is charged. Pressing it opens Edit, Duplicate and Delete under it.

## Usage

<Canvas of={Examples.Basic} sourceState="shown" />

One per expense in the `CardList` of a day on its `Sheet`. `expenses` is the expense, or every part of its plan, so Edit and Delete always get the whole of it. `source` names the group of a row that is the person's part of a shared expense.

## Without text

The name of the category stands in for it, and the quiet line goes.
