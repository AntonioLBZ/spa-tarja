# CategoryRow

A category the person made, a row on the sheet "Yours" of the categories screen: its badge and its name. Pressing it opens Edit and Delete under it; Delete asks first, and says where its expenses go (`fallbackLabel`).

## Usage

<Canvas of={Examples.Basic} sourceState="shown" />

One per category of the person's own, in a `CardList` with `flush` on a `Sheet`. Edit loads the category into the form that adds one; `onDelete` resolves whether it went.
