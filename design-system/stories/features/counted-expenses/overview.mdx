# CountedExpenses

What the charts count, expense by expense, at the foot of the charts: a ticket for each row of the chart that has any, its total being that column. The rows only read: what is done to an expense is done in the list. They come 100 at a time, with Show more.

## Usage

<Canvas of={Examples.Basic} sourceState="shown" />

`groups` comes from `countedExpenses` (`@core/reports/charts`) with the range, the grouping and the category the chart has. An expense counts on the day it is charged, so a card payment made in September shows in October. `pendingIds` marks what waits in the outbox, and `sourceOf` names the group of a shared row.

## By month

The tickets follow the rows of the chart: by day, by week or by month.
