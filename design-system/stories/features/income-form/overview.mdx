# IncomeForm

The income of a month, on the Summary: one `Field` named after the month with Save beside it, and Remove once the month has one. Saving and removing need a connection.

## Usage

<Canvas of={Examples.Basic} sourceState="shown" />

`month` and `monthName` are the month of the Summary; `income` is what that month holds, `undefined` for none. The field starts again from it whenever it changes: another month, a save, a removal.
