# ExpenseForm

The form of an expense: For (Just me or Shared) in tabs, the amount, the text and the category, and folded under More options the day, the payment and the installments; a shared one adds Split, with who it is with, who paid and who is in. The category is suggested from the text and the history.

## Usage

<Canvas of={Examples.Basic} sourceState="shown" />

It lives in the sheet the "+" opens (`ExpenseSheet`), whose foot holds the button that sends it, named by the form's `id`; here a button under the form stands for it. Add is never off: pressing it marks each problem on its field and focuses the first. `onAdded` gets the day of a new expense, and `onDone` follows an edit or a copy.

## Editing

`editing` takes the expense, or every part of its plan, and the button saves it.
