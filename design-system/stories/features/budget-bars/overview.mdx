# BudgetBars

How much of each limit a month has used, on the Summary: the total first, then each category that has a limit, a bar under the words. A limit passed shows its share and its bar in `--color-danger`; it only warns, it never blocks.

## Usage

<Canvas of={Examples.Basic} sourceState="shown" />

`month` is the month of the Summary, `YYYY-MM`. The limits are read as that month holds them: the row of the latest month up to it. A month with no limit draws nothing.
