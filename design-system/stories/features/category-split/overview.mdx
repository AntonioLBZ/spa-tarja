# CategorySplit

What each category took in the charts: one strip cut into a segment per category in its color, and under it a row for each with its badge, name, amount and share. The biggest comes first.

## Usage

<Canvas of={Examples.Basic} sourceState="shown" />

Under the chart of Spending. Each row is a toggle that picks its category, one at a time, and narrows the chart to it; pressing it again lets go. `onEmptied` takes the focus when the row that had it goes away with nothing left to take it.

## Picked

The picked row is pressed, and the others stay to pick another.
