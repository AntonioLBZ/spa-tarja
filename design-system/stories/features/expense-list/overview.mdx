# ExpenseList

The Expenses screen: the search and the filters button over the person's expenses, grouped by the day they were made, each day on a `Sheet` with its total. A row opens to Edit, Duplicate and Delete; a plan of installments is one row at its full price.

## Usage

<Canvas of={Examples.Basic} sourceState="shown" />

It reads the person's expenses, their part of the shared ones and their categories. It goes under a `ToastProvider` (the Undo of a delete) and the `ExpenseSheetProvider` (Edit and Duplicate open in the sheet of the add form). `titleHidden` keeps its title for assistive technology alone while the pinned groups are tabs over it.
