# YourData

The section Your data of Settings → Account: download what the device holds as JSON or CSV, open the printable view of the person's expenses, and read the privacy notice.

## Usage

<Canvas of={Examples.Basic} sourceState="shown" />

It takes no props: it exports the expenses, categories, income, budgets and groups the device holds. Import stays out of the app.
