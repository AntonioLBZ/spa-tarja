# SpendingChart

Spending over time, in the charts: a column for each day, week or month on a round scale, the biggest and today's marked by shape, a readout of the column picked by a tap or the arrow keys, and the numbers in a table under Show the numbers for whoever can't see the columns.

## Usage

<Canvas of={Examples.Basic} sourceState="shown" />

`buckets` are the totals of the range, from `@core/reports/charts`, grouped by `granularity`. `today` marks today's column.
