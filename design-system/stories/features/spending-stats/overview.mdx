# SpendingStats

The line of the total of the charts, over the chart: what the range took and how it compares with the span before, by the calendar ("1–29 Oct vs. 1–29 Sep").

## Usage

<Canvas of={Examples.Basic} sourceState="shown" />

`totalCents` is the total of the range and `compared` the two spans side by side. The total can take the focus (`totalRef`), as when a pick of the split changes it.
